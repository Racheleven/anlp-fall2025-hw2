a person shall be deemed to be conducting business within the city if one engages, hires, employs or contracts with one or more individuals as employees, partners, or is self-employed and, in addition, does at least one of the following: (1) maintains a fixed place of business within the city; (2) owns or leases real property within the city for purposes of such business; (3) maintains a stock of tangible, personal property in the city for sale in the ordinary course of business; (4) conducts continuous solicitation within the city related to such business; or (5) utilizes the streets of the city in connection with the operation of such business, other than for the mere transportation from a site outside the city, through the city, to a destination outside of the city. a p
