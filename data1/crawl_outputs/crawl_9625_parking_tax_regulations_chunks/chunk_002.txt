clause and severability. ......................................... 28 -3- parking tax regulations -4- introduction the pittsburgh code, title two, fiscal, chapter 253, imposes a tax for general revenue purposes upon each parking transaction by a patron of a non-residential parking place, within the city of pittsburgh, at the rate set by ordinance on the consideration for each transaction. this tax is levied pursuant to the authority granted by the local tax enabling act, act 511 of 1965, 53 p.s. § 6924.301.1, and the city of pittsburgh home rule charter of 1974, excluding exceptions as defined in section 253.01(b). the tax rate on parking transactions may not exceed the ceiling set forth in municipal pension plan funding standard and recovery act, 53 p.s. § 895.902(a). the tax is based upon the entire consideration paid by the patron, without deduction for any costs or expenses directly or indirectly related to these receipts. inquiries relevant to specific business operations
