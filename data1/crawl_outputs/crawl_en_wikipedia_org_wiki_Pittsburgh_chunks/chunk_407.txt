[ ] according to a 2010 association of religion data archives (arda) study, residents included 773,341 "catholics"; 326,125 "mainline protestants"; 174,119 "evangelical protestants;" 20,976 "black protestants;" and 16,405 "orthodox christians," with 996,826 listed as "unclaimed" and 16,405 as "other" in the metro area. a 2017 study by the cohen center for modern jewish studies at brandeis university estimated the jewish population of greater pittsburgh was 49,200.
