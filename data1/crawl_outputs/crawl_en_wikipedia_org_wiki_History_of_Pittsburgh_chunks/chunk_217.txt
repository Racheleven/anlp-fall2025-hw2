[ ] germans[edit] germans [edit] [ edit ] during the mid-19th century, pittsburgh witnessed a dramatic influx of german immigrants, including a brick mason whose son, henry j. heinz, founded the h.j. heinz company in 1869. heinz was at the forefront of reform efforts to improve food purity, working conditions, hours, and wages, but the company bitterly opposed the formation of an independent labor union.
