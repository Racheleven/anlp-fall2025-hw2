# CMU125
Make plans to attend Homecoming Weekend, Nov. 6-8, to celebrate 125 years of Carnegie Mellon University. In addition to traditional festivities like Spirit Day and the football game, CMU125 at Homecoming Weekend[https://www.cmu.edu/125/celebration-weekend/index.html]features special events and opportunities for Tartans of all ages to celebrate this remarkable moment in CMU history.
