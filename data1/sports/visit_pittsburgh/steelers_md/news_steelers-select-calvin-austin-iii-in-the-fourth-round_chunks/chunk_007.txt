# PHOTOS: Austin III in pictures

Austin said he doesn't know how the Steelers will use him in their offensive scheme but is willing to do whatever offensive coordinator Matt Canada wants him to do.  
"I am just ready to come in and for him to use me however is needed," said Austin. "I am that guy that can play inside, outside, jet sweeps, I can do it all. Whatever role he needs me, I will be excited to go out there and do that job."  
Austin is listed as 5-9, 162 on his bio, and said that his size isn't something that is an issue, and he brings physicality and a fearless approach, something he credits to his family.  
"Growing up is where I became physical," said Austin. "I had a bunch of older cousins, and we would go to the park and play tackle football all of the time. From that point on I was tough because they wouldn't take it easy on me. I liked to show off my toughness.  
"I am never scared when I am on the field. When I am on the field, I feel like I am the biggest receiver out there."
