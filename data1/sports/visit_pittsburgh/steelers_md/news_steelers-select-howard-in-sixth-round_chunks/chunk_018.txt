# PHOTOS: Will Howard in pictures

2021: Academic All-Big 12 ... Played in six games with starts against Nevada, Oklahoma State and Texas ... Finished the season throwing for 332 yards and a TD, while he rushed 32 times for 184 yards and four TDs ... Had a career-high two rushing TDs against Nevada, both of which came in the fourth quarter as K-State broke a 17-17 tie... Tallied a 71- yard TD run at Texas, the longest for a score in his career and the second-longest overall (80 yards at TCU, 2020) ... Tossed a 68-yard TD pass against Nevada, the second of his career over 65 yards.
