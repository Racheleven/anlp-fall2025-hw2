# PHOTOS: Will Howard in pictures

PERSONAL: Entered the portal in December 2023 after a stellar, four-year career at Kansas State, and transferred to Ohio State in January of 2024 ... Rated the 13th-best pro-style quarterback in the 2020 recruiting class by 247Sports ... Named the All-Southeastern Pennsylvania Player of the Year by the Philadelphia Inquirer, all-state by the Pennsylvania Football Writers and the Maxwell Football Club Pennsylvania Player of the Year ... Threw for 5,308 yards and 48 TDs during his prep career ... A 1,000-plus point scorer as a prep basketball player ... Son of Maureen and Bob Howard and has three siblings: Tori, Ryan and Grace.
