# PHOTOS: Will Howard in pictures

2024: Started all 16 games at quarterback for Ohio State in 2024 and led the team to a school-record 14-win season and the CFP National Championship ... Set Ohio State single-season and career completion percentage records by completing 71.4 percent of his passes (325-of-455) ... Set Ohio State record with eight games with at least an 80 percent completion percentage ... His 325 completions, 455 attempts and 4,208 passing yards all rank third in Ohio State single- season history ... Offensive MVP in the Cotton Bowl win over Texas and the CFP National Championship game win over Notre Dame ... Quarterbacked Ohio State to five victories over Top 5-ranked teams, an NCAA record: over No. 3 Penn State, No. 5 Indiana, No. 1 Oregon, No. 3 Texas and No. 5 Notre Dame ... Also led Ohio State to a sixth Top 10 win, over No. 7 Tennessee to open the College Football Playoffs, by throwing for 311 yards and two TDs in a 42-17 rout that launched the Buckeyes on their championship run of wins ...
