Art Rooney Sr. was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 1965. Rooney was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
He wasn't the first in the Steelers organization to be inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame, and that is just how Rooney would have wanted it.  
In his mind, the players and coaches always came first, and Rooney would happily remain in the background, giving credit to those around him.
