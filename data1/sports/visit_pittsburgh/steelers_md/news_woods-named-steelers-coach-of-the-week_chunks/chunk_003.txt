#  Woods named Steelers Coach of the Week  
Sep 23, 2025 at 02:00 PM

**Steelers Coach of the Week - Week 4**  
**Ed Woods, California High School**  
Week 4 Coach of the Week goes to Coach Ed Woods of the California Trojans. Woods led his team to a 42-0 shutout victory over Avella. The Trojans were productive both on the ground and through the air, highlighted by a 67-yard touchdown pass, against a stingy Avella defense that had allowed only 24 points all season entering the matchup. This marks Coach Woods' first Coach of the Week honor since taking over the program in 2024.
