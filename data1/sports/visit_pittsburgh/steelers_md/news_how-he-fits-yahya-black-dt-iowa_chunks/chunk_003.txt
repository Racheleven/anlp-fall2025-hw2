At just a shade under 6-foot-6 and 336 pounds, Black is an impressive-looking athlete to add to the defensive line room.  
The Steelers view Black as a player capable of playing defensive end in their 3-4 defense â along with first-round pick Derrick Harmon â and lessening the load on All-Pro Cam Heyward.
