#  Lehmeier named Steelers Coach of the Week  
Sep 02, 2025 at 01:00 PM  
*   *   *   * Copied!  
**Steelers Coach of the Week - Week 1**  
**Ryan Lehmeier, Central Catholic High School**  
Week 1 Coach of the Week goes to Coach Ryan Lehmeier from the Central Catholic Vikings. Lehmeier led the Vikings to a 34-20 victory over Pine-Richland, highlighted by a tough Central Catholic defense that forced 3 turnovers and held the Rams scoreless in the second half. This win snapped a 3-game drought against Pine-Richland and marks Coach Lehmeier's first Coach of the Week honor since taking over the program in 2023.
