## Biography

* Made NFL starting debut in Week 3 of 2013 (vs. Chicago)  
**2024 (STEELERS)**  
* Played in and started all 17 games in 2024
* Named a team captain for the 10th consecutive season
* Finished the season with 71 tackles (35 solo), 20 QB hits, 12 tackles for loss, 8.0 sacks, and led the team with a career-high 11 passes defensed
* His 11 passes defensed were the most by any front seven defender since 2021 (Emmanuel Ogbah, 12)
* At age 35, recieved the second-highest PFF grade among all interior defenders in 2024 (90.1) trailing only Kansas City's Chris Jones
* Recieved his fourth AP first-team All-Pro nod and seveth Pro Bowl nod, and finished 13th in voting for AP Defensive Player of the Year and ninth in voting for AP Comeback Player of the Year
* By the end of the season, played in 211 career games, surpassing Donnie Shell (201) and Mel Blount (200) for the most games played amongst all defensive players in franchise history
