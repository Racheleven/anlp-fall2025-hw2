# Jerome Bettis Leadership Award | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

####  
Tommy Wuycheck  
**Seneca Valley High School**  
**_Senior | OL/DL_**  
Tommy is the ideal role model for @SVRaiderFB and his community. He is a member of Seneca Valley's Student-Athlete Leadership Team, a team captain for the football team, and brings a contagious positive energy every day that those around him follow. He is also heavily involved in community service activities such as St. Ferdinand's White Elephant Sale and assisting with local youth football leagues and summer camps. Tommy is a selfless young man who is quick to direct praise to his teammates and coaches.
