##  HIGH SCHOOL BOY'S FOOTBALL  
####  
Jayden Salada  
**New Brighton High School**  
**_Senior | QB/DB_**  
Jayden is an exceptional leader at New Brighton who positively influences his teammates by leading through example both on and off the football field. Beyond his role at the local YMCA, he stands out as a peer leader and, most notably, ranks in the top 10 of his class.  
####  
Amari Eleam  
**Carrick High School**  
**_Senior | WR/DB_**  
Amari leads by example both on and off the field at Carrick High School, demonstrating his commitment through his leadership in the Raider Walk, where he actively participates in cleaning up local neighborhoods. Additionally, he volunteers as a youth flag football referee for the Pittsburgh Flag Football League.
