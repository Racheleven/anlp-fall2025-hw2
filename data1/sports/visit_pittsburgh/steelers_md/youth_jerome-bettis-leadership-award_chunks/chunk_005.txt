# Jerome Bettis Leadership Award | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

####  
Charles Simmons  
**Bethel Park High School**  
**_Senior | FB/LB_**  
Charles is an exemplary leader in his community and on his football team. He prides himself on integrity, inclusivity, and giving back to others by showing younger players the ropes and leading by example both on and off the field. Charles is also active in his community, extending his influence off the field as an active member of the Best Buddies Club where he works with and develops connections with students who have special needs and disabilities. He is also heavily involved as a volunteer within his church.
