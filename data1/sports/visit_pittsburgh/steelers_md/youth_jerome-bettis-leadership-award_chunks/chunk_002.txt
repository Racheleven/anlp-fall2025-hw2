# Jerome Bettis Leadership Award | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
Student athletes have a unique opportunity to offer mentoring support to their peers to help them learn and grow on AND off the field! The Pittsburgh Steelers and The Mentoring Partnership are teaming up with The Pittsburgh Post-Gazette and Giant Eagle to present the Jerome Bettis Leadership Award, featuring high school football players from around the region who go above and beyond to provide mentor-like support for their teammates. Each player receives:  
1. An on-field celebration at a Steelers home game
2. A $1,000 donation to their school's football program
3. Recognition on Steelers social media channels
