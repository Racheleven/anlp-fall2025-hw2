# Jerome Bettis Leadership Award | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

2025 WINNERS  
####  
Connor Hiser  
**South Fayette High School**  
**_Senior | OL_**  
Connor is a true leader for his football team and community. He is an active leader in school as a member of the National Honor Society, as well as an active member of his community. Connor volunteered at the Veterans Rock Concert, the Miracle League of Pittsburgh, and volunteers to mentor all of South Fayettes youth football and elementary school activities. Connor is always willing to take on a leadership role to help mentor others no matter the situation.
