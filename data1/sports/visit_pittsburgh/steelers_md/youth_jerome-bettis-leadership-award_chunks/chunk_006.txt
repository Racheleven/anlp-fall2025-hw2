# Jerome Bettis Leadership Award | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

####  
Max Topper  
**Greensburg Salem High School**  
**_Senior | WR/LB_**  
Max is an excellent leader for Greensburg Salem's football team, as well as in his community. He spends his spare time mentoring youth football players, and volunteering for roadside clean-up projects, events supporting victims of domestic violence, and working with local businesses to connect sports programs in the community. Max is a captain of the football team, but his contributions as a leader don't stop there, as he is also the President of National Honor Society, and a member on the Leadership Council.
