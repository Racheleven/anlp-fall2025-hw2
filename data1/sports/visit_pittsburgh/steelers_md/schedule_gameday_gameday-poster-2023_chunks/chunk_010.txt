**Bio:** Hi, my name is Shawn Atkins and I'm a cartoonist of webcomics, a lover of oatmeal raisin cookies, and a dad. Comics have been a lifelong passion of mine. From exploring the depths of the ocean to the outer reaches of time and space, comics fuel my curiosity about what's out there in the world. Recently re-found my love of Photography and the Darkroom at Pittsburgh Center of the Arts where I offer comic-making classes. When I'm not making comics I'm hanging with my family, watching movies, cooking, or opening a portal to the dark dimension like one does on a Tuesday morning. Currently, I'm going back to school to become an art teacher.
