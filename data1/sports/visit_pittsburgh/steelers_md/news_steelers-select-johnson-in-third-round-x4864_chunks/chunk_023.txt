# PHOTOS: Kaleb Johnson in pictures

2022: Honorable mention Freshman All-America by College Football News ... Honorable mention All-Big Ten by league coaches and media ... Named Big Ten Conference Offensive Player of the Week and Freshman of the Week for his play in win at Purdue ... Saw action in all 13 games, starting six games ... Rushed 151 times for 779 yards (5.2 yard avg.) and six TDs, which led the team ... Returned 13 kickoffs for 325 yards (25.0 yard avg.), which also led the team ... His 779 rushing yards ranks 37th for most yards in a season in program history and set an Iowa freshman record ... Rushed 22 times for season-high 200 yards and one TD in 24-3 win at Purdue, which was the second highest single-game total by an Iowa freshman and 18th best total in program history ... His 75-yard TD run on second play of third quarter was longest for Iowa since 2020 ... First true freshman to rush for over 100 yards in a game since 2019 (Tyler Goodson) ... Led rushing attack with 22 carries for 57 yards in 24-10
