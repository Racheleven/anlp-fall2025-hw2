## New Additions  
#### **The museum is featuring a new rotating exhibit highlighting the anniversary of Super Bowl XL and the Steelers 2005 season. This interactive display details the long road to the team's 5th Super Bowl win, and to highlight the display - the Super Bowl XL Lombardi Trophy for fans' viewing pleasure.**  
####  
Hours of Operation & Pricing  
The Steelers Hall of Honor Museum is located above the Steelers Pro Shop at Acrisure Stadium and accessible via the Gate B suite entry. Fans can visit the Steelers Hall of Honor Museum year-round, Wednesday-Monday, from 11 a.m. - 4 p.m. Now offering hours on select game days. The museum is closed on Tuesday, and dates and hours are subject to change without notice.
