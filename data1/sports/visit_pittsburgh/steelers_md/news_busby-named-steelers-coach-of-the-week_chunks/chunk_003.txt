**Steelers Coach of the Week - Week 5**  
**Ron Busby, Western Beaver High School**  
Week 5 Coach of the Week honors go to Coach Ron Busby of the Western Beaver Golden Beavers. Busby led his team to a 48-19 conference victory over Riverside behind a strong rushing attack, going for over 250 yards and 7 touchdowns. Coach Busby has led the Golden Beavers to a 6-0 start for the first time in 20 years and remains one of only nine undefeated WPIAL teams. This marks Coach Busby's first Coach of the Week honors since taking over the program in 2021.
