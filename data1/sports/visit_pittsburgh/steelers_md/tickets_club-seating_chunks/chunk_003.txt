## Club Seating Information

####  
North Club Lounge  
As the smallest club space in Acrisure Stadium, the North Club Lounge provides a more private atmosphere on gameday. The ticketed seating options include two rows (rows A and B) of outdoor seats and Acrisure Stadium's only indoor row of seats (row C). The North Club Lounge access includes the indoor lounge space, Ã la carte concessions, main bar and restrooms. From the lounge space, guests will have a spectacular view of the field, main scoreboard and downtown Pittsburgh from the third level of the stadium.
