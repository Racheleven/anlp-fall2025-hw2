UPMC Club Lounge  
The UPMC Club Lounge extends across the eastern half of Acrisure Stadium from Gate B to Gate A East. From the indoor lounge space, guests can view downtown Pittsburgh through the two-story glass windows while enjoying Ã la carte concession options, the modern-style main bar and soft-seating lounge areas. The club space also includes restrooms and merchandise items. Enjoy a pregame snack in the air-conditioning during sunny gamedays or escape the cold for a time-out toward the end of the season. The UPMC Club Lounge has a sister club, the West Club Lounge, which can also be accessed with a UPMC Club Lounge ticket.
