# PHOTOS: Leal in pictures

Leal said he was aware the Steelers had an interest in him after interaction with defensive line coach Karl Dunbar during the pre-draft process.  
"We hit it off, talked good ball, and talked to my family, and I could tell they were interested as well," said Leal. "Talking to all the coaches at the combine, a day full of meetings. I'm genuinely excited to be a part of the organization.  
"Everything worked out the way I wanted to at the end of the day. I'm just glad to be in this position and be a part of the Pittsburgh Steelers."
