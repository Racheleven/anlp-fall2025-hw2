STEELERS HIGH SCHOOL FOOTBALL SHOWCASE TO BE FEATURED ON KDKA+  
The High School Showcase Game of the Week, presented by Giant Eagle and the Pittsburgh Post-Gazette, will return to the airwaves for the 2025 season, but it will be bigger and better than ever before as the team continues to build on its support of youth and high school football in the Western Pennsylvania area.  
For the second straight season, the Steelers will elevate the High School Showcase each week by broadcasting the featured game on KDKA+. The season kicks off on August 22 in Clairton with the grand opening of Tyler Boyd Stadium, hosting Imani Christian at 7 p.m.
