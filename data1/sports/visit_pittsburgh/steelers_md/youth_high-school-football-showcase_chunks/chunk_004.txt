## Steelers High School Football Showcase

The Steelers High School Showcase Game of the Week first launched in 2018. The program is one on a list of endeavors the Steelers have undertaken to help in the education and growth of youth football. For more information and details on coverage of youth and high school football, follow **@SteelersYouthFB** on X, **@steelersyouthFB** on Instagram and **steelersyouthfb** on Snapchat.  
For the second straight season, the Steelers partner with JRM Video Production and will be featuring the announcing team of Matt Farago alongside Steelers Legends, Mike Logan, Arthur Moats, and sideline reporter Suzie Cool.
