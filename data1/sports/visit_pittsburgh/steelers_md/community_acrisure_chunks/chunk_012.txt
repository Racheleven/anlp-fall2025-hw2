# Acrisure Assists Community | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

Pittsburgh Public Schools  
**Summary of organization:** The Pittsburgh Public School District is the largest of 43 school districts in Allegheny County and second largest in Pennsylvania. The District provides students, in PreK through Grade 12, a variety of options to help them succeed in school and prepare them for college, career, and life.  
**What does this partnership mean to your organization:** This partnership means more than free Steelers tickets for Pittsburgh Public Schools families and staff, elevating eight important factors:  
1. Financial Relief: NFL game tickets can be expensive, and for many families, attending a live game might be financially challenging. Free tickets provide an opportunity for families who may not have the means to enjoy such events, offering a break from the financial strain. We had several families that attended a Steelers game for the first time due to Acrisure's generosity.
