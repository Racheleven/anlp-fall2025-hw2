Sample Supports  
**Summary of organization:** Sample Supports provides comprehensive social services to youth and adults with intellectual and developmental disabilities in community-based settings. Our core mission is to raise the bar of service delivery by providing superior and competitive community-based care options to the people most in need in our communities.  
**What does this partnership mean to your organization:** A partnership with the Steelers and Acrisure means that we are truly living our mission of being an integrated part of the local and national community. We love the Acrisure and Steelers dedication to including people of diverse needs and backgrounds and they have leaned in with open arms to include the people we serve.
