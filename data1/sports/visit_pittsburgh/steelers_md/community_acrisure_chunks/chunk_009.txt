YouthPlaces  
**Summary of organization:** YouthPlaces creates safe spaces and experiences that inspire young people to imagine, explore, and design their own futures. Servicing youth in grades 6 - 12, YouthPlaces provides after-school programming in the communities that are under resourced and deemed hardest to reach. We understand success takes no single form, and our steadfast vision for our 7 after-school locations, is that youth in our communities become happy, healthy, and dynamic adults.  
**What does this partnership mean to your organization:** The partnership with Acrisure means more experiences for the teens we serve annually. We are huge advocates of changing a youth's environment and watching how their minds begin to expand beyond the limits that were created. With Acrisure providing those opportunities, we can continue to create lifelong memories throughout our communities!
