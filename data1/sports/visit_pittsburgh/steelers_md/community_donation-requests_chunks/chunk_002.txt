# Steelers Donation Requests | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
####  
Steelers Donation Requests  
The Pittsburgh Steelers are committed to supporting a wide range of charities in our community. Our goal is to accommodate as many requests as possible. Please keep in mind that this platform is used to evaluate **charitable donation requests only.**  
**Please be sure to read the guidelines below before submitting your request.**  
1. Please submit **requests at least 6** **(six) weeks** prior to the event date to be considered.
2. Requests for **game** **ticket donations** will **not** be accepted.
3. We **do not accept unsolicited items** and will not assume responsibility for items mailed to the facility.
4. Employee recognition events, company picnics, and family reunions **will not be accepted**.
5. Organizations are only eligible to receive **one** **(1) donation per calendar year**.
