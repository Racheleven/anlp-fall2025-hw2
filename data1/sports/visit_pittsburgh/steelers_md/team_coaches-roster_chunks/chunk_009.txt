#####  Head Strength and Conditioning Coach  
Phil Matusz is in his second season as the Steelersâ head strength and conditioning coach in 2025.  
Matusz came to Pittsburgh after a four-year stint as the head football strength and conditioning coach for Boston College (2020-23). He oversaw the physical and weight training development of four NFL Draft picks, including first-round selections Zion Johnson (2022) and Zay Flowers (2023).
