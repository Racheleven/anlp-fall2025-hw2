STEELERS VEBT RELIEF POWERED BY VERIZON  
This season, Verizon and the Pittsburgh Steelers are teaming up to make a difference in the lives of veterans throughout Western Pennsylvania. **The Steelers VEBT Relief Program, presented by Verizon, will erase $100,000 of debt for local veterans every time the Steelers win.**  
Using ForgiveCo, to leverage industry expertise and find veterans in need, this initiative will make a massive difference for those who have served all of us. Throughout Southwestern Pennsylvania alone, local veterans carry more than $10 million worth of debt. No applications are needed and veterans selected for relief will be notified through ForgiveCo's random acts of kindness model after each Steelers win.  
This year, we're all waving our towels to help cheer on an initiative that will bring relief to those who have served our country.
