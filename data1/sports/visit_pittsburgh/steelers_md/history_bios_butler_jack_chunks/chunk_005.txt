After his retirement from the Steelers, Butler went on to a career in scouting, and served as the executive director for BLESTO.  
But it was that day in Canton that was among the best in Butler's life.  
"I never envisioned being here in Canton," said Butler. "This induction is the highest honor I have achieved in my professional career. I have always been grateful and happy to have played, having a 56-year career doing the things I always loved, being involved in professional football â as a player, coach, scout and administrator. I am honored to be included with the great players who preceded me here.
