# Steelers select Kenny Pickett in the first round

**_Virginia (Nov. 20):_** Â In one of the grittiest performances of his career, Pickett threw for 340 yards and four touchdowns in lifting Pitt to a Coastal Division-clinching 48-38 victory over Virginia. Pickett left the contest due to injury and acknowledged playing through discomfort during the game's final crucial moments, but threw a 62-yard touchdown strike with two minutes left to seal the win and the Coastal title.  
**_North Carolina (Nov. 11):_** Â Pickett directed Pitt to a 30-23 overtime victory over North Carolina by throwing for 346 yards and three touchdowns on 25 of 43 passing (58%). He had one interception. In the extra period, played under a heavy downpour, Pickett produced the winning points by throwing a dart to Lucas Krull for an 11-yard score. The victory over UNC marked Pickett's third career win in overtime.
