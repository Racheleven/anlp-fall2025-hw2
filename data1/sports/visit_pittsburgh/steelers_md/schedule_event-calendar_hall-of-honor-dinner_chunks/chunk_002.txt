# Hall of Honor Induction Ceremony | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
Our annual Hall of Honor Dinner, presented by U.S. Steel, will celebrate the induction of this year's Hall of Honor class during a special ceremony at Acrisure Stadium. The evening features drinks, dinner, and the appearance of Steelers Legends. The 2025 Class includes three individuals who were part of Super Bowl seasons for the Steelers - center Maurkice Pouncey, linebacker Joey Porter, and quarterback Ben Roethlisberger.
