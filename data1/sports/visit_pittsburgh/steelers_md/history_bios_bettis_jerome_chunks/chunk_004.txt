# Jerome Bettis | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

His NFL playing career ended with a trip back home to Detroit, for Super Bowl XL where he hoisted the Lombardi Trophy, but the excitement wasn't over for Jerome Bettis. The Steelers all-everything running back is part of football immortality as he was elected to the Pro Football Hall of Fame in 2015.  
Steelers Nation descended upon Canton, Ohio for his enshrinement, and Bettis thanked them, as well as teammates and others, in his speech.  
"I want to thank you all for appreciating a power running game â 3 yards and a cloud of dust was far better than a 40-yard bomb down the field," Bettis told the crowd. "Thank you for embracing me and my entire family as your own. But thank you most importantly for your support not only of me but my entire team as we went out and played a game we loved and knew we had the support of the best football fans in the world.
