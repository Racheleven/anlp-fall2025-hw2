**Bio:** Pittsburgh-based artist Jay Nesbitt whose career spans over three decades, specializing in painting, drawing, mixed media, and installation art. His art oscillates between representational and conceptual. This synergy allows him to transform everyday items into creative pieces infused with humor and insight, resonating with art enthusiasts and casual viewers alike.  
Nesbitt, a Carnegie Mellon University graduate, seeks to make the mundane wondrous, encouraging viewers to see the everyday through a new artistic lens. Nesbitt's art not only seeks to captivate visually but also challenges perceptions, underscoring the transformative impact of art. His work can be found in galleries and private collections across the country.
