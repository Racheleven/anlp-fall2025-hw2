**Bio:** Mike Budai is an illustrator and screen printer working in Pittsburgh, Pa. He began working in screen shops in the mid 90's while attending Edinboro University for Animation and Printmaking. Upon completing college, he began volunteering at Artists Image Resource (A.I.R.) in 2001 working in various mediums, primarily screen printing. Since then, he has created hundreds of silk-screened posters for concerts, logos and illustrations for businesses, non-profits and organizations, and has shown his print and illustration work in galleries all over the US, including Mondo in Austin, Tx., Rotofugi in Chicago Il., and Gallery 1988 in Los Angeles.
