## 2024 Gameday Posters

Her favorite projects to work on allow her to bring her individual style to help tell someone's story, or to explain the significance of beloved places. Since 2017, she has been steadily working on a personal illustration project called 100 Days of Pittsburgh (#100daysofpittsburgh on Instagram), where she illustrates 100 people, places and landmarks connected to Pittsburgh. This has been invaluable in finding her creative voice and personal style, and has allowed her to work with local clients who have a shared interest.
