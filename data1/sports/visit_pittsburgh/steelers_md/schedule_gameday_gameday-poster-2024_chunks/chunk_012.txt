Week 5: Steelers vs. Cowboys  
**Bio:** Brian Dumm is a professional artist and art educator from Ebensburg, Pennsylvania where he works and lives with his wife and two daughters. Brian is represented by Storybook Arts Inc. in New York, and his award-winning illustrations have been featured in over a hundred publications globally since 2010. He has completed artwork for such notable clients as Scholastic, McGraw-Hill, and the Sheetz corporation.  
Brian earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from Penn State University in 2000 and a master's degree in Drawing and Painting from Indiana University of Pennsylvania in 2013. His fine-arts drawing/paintings have shown in galleries through Pennsylvania, Ohio, and Maryland.
