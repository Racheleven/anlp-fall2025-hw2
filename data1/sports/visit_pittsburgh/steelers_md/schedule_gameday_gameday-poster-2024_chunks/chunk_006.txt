Week 14: Steelers vs. Browns  
**Bio:** Seth Storck is a user experience designer & illustrator living and working in Pittsburgh, PA.  
Seth grew up in central PA where everyone he knows roots for the Steelers. He started drawing at a very young age and has never stopped. He moved to Pittsburgh in 2002 to attend the Art Institute to study graphic/web design.  
He has worked on creative projects for Sheetz, Flatrock Brewing Co, Rusty Waters Apparel, Grounded, The Trade Institute of Pittsburgh, Crazy Mocha and various bands and events.  
Outside of client work, Seth has shown his artwork in galleries and art shows. He has illustrated two children's books, two adult coloring books and a handful of comic books.  
Fun fact: Seth shaved the Steelers logo into the back of his head for the 1995 Super Bowl.
