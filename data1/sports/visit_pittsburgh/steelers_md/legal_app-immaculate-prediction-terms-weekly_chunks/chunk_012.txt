# Steelers Immaculate Prediction Terms & Conditions | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

**WINNERS:** There will be three (3) winners (each, a "**Winner** ") selected on September 29, 2025. The three (3) Winners will be chosen from the pool of eligible Entrants who have completed the required steps as of the end of the Contest Period and are top three (3) highest ranked Entrants on the leadership board. In the case of a tie, the Winners will be randomly selected from those Entrants who are tied for the lead for the Contest. Winning is subject to verification of eligibility. Winning the Contest shall not preclude an Entrant from winning the VIP Contest.
