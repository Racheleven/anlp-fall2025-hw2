# GAME PHOTOS: Week 1 at Jets

The Jets answered with a 33-yard touchdown pass from Justin Fields to Garrett Wilson. Because of a Steelers formation penalty on the missed kick, the Jets attempted a two-point conversion, but Braelon Allen was stopped and the Jets took their 9-7 lead into the second quarter.  
Boswell followed with his 56-yarder to put the Steelers ahead 10-9 with 13:26 left in the first half. The key play of the drive was a 31-yard Rodgers screen pass to Metcalf.  
The offensive show by both teams continued as the Jets answered with another touchdown, an 8-yard run by Allen with 10:09 left in the half. The extra point put the Jets ahead, 16-10.  
The Jets then forced the first Steelers punt of the game, and took advantage with a 51-yard field goal by Folk. The kick extended the Jets' lead to 19-10 with 2:54 remaining in the half.
