The Jets opened the scoring with a 35-yard field goal by Nick Folk to cap their first drive. Breece Hall ripped off an 18-yard run on his first carry as the Jets advanced to the Pittsburgh 19. But a tackle by Alex Highsmith, a tackle-for-loss by T.J. Watt, and a tackle by Patrick Queen forced the Jets to kick the field goal with 9:15 left in the opening quarter.  
The Steelers answered with Rodgers' first touchdown pass with the Steelers, a 22-yard play-action throw to a wide open Skowronek for his first touchdown with the Steelers. It put the Steelers ahead 7-3 with 3:38 left in the first.
