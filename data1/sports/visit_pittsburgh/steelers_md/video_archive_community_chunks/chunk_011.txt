###  WATCH: Austin receives Ed Block Courage Award  
Steelers wide receiver Calvin Austin III received the Ed Block Courage Award at the 31st Annual Arthur J. Rooney, Sr. Courage House Luncheon at Acrisure Stadium. The award is presented annually to a Steelers player who has fought back from an injury or tough circumstance to return to the field.
