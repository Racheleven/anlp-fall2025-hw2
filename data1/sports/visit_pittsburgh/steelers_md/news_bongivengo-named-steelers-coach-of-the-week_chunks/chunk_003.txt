#  Bongivengo named Steelers Coach of the Week  
Sep 09, 2025 at 01:00 PM  
*   *   *   * Copied!  
**Steelers Coach of the Week - Week 2**  
**Michael Bongivengo, Neshannock High School**  
Week 2 Coach of the Week goes to Coach Michael Bongivengo from @NeshannockFB. Bongivengo led the Lancers to a 26-20 victory over Ellwood City, highlighted by a late-game touchdown pass with just 13 seconds left on the clock. The Lancers' defense forced key turnovers in the second half, while the offense battled back to seal the win. This is Coach Bongivengo's first Coach of the Week recognition since taking over the program in 2024."
