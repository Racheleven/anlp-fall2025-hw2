## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What should I do if I do not have a smartphone or if my phone is lost, breaks or dies before I get to the stadium?  
Please visit our Customer Service Windows located on Art Rooney Avenue for assistance. Customer Service windows open 3 hours prior to kickoff.  
What if my smartphone is outdated and I'm having issues accessing my tickets?  
If you have an outdated smartphone model, some functionality with the Steelers app or Account Manager may not be available. If you experience any issues with this, please contact us prior to gameday at 412-323-1200 or visit the Ticket Office windows which open 3 hours prior to kick-off on gameday.
