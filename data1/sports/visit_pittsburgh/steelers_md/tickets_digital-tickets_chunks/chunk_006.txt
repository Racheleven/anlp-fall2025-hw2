##  ACCEPTING TICKETS SENT TO YOU  
1\. Tap "Accept Tickets" in the email you received or tap the link in the text message you receive.  
2\. Sign in with your Steelers Account Manager email and password. If you don't have one, you can easily create one by tapping "sign up"  
3\. Once accepted, be sure to save your tickets in your digital wallet.
