# Mobile Ticketing | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
####  
Your Steelers Mobile App just got an upgrade! We've partnered with Ticketmaster to make improvements to your mobile experience.  
Your Steelers Mobile App is better than ever! To access your tickets and enjoy the newest features and enhancements, you must have the latest version. Update your app by following these steps:  
**For an Apple device:**
Open the App Store.
Tap your profile icon at the top of the screen.
Scroll to see pending updates and release notes. Tap Update next to the Steelers mobile app.  
**For an Android device:**
Open the Google Play Store app.
Tap the profile icon on the top right.
Tap Manage apps & device. Apps with an update available are labeled "Update available."
Tap Update next to the Steelers mobile app.
_Android users_ **_must_** _have their NFC reader turned on in order for your barcode to scan at the gate._
