##  SELL YOUR TICKETS  
1\. Select the game you would like to sell and tap "Sell"  
2\. Select the seats you wish to sell then tap "Set Price"  
3\. Set the sale price per ticket then scroll down to "Payout Method"  
4\. Select your payout method  
5\. Confirm the information and click "List Tickets"
