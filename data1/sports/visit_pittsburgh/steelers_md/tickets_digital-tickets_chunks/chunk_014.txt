## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

When I have issues with my mobile tickets, who can I contact?  
Fans with question or needing further help should contact us at 412-323-1200. Our office hours are Monday through Friday 9 am-5 pm and on gameday 3 hours prior to kickoff. Or visit the Ticket Office windows located at the corner of Art Rooney Avenue and West General Robinson on gameday starting 3 hours prior to kickoff.
