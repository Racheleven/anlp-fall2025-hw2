##  ACCESS AND SAVE YOUR TICKETS  
1\. Open the Steelers Official Mobile App  
2\. Tap "tickets" in the bottom navigation then "My Tickets"  
3\. Tap "Sign In to Steelers" at the bottom  
4\. Sign in using the email address and password associated with your Steelers Account Manager and Ticketmaster account  
5\. Select the game  
6\. Tap "Add to Wallet" or "Save to phone" and confirm which tickets to add. Once complete, your tickets will be accessible within your phone's wallet.
