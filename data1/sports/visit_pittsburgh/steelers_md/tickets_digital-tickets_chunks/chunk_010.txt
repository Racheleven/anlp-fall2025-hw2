##  HOW TO DOWNLOAD THE STEELERS APP TO ACCESS YOUR TICKETS  
1\. Download the Steelers Official Mobile App from the Apple or Google Play Store  
2\. Open the app then hit "Continue"  
3\. Select whether you would like to receive notifications  
4\. Next, create a Fan Profile to customize your Steelers app experience. Or, select "Skip." **IMPORTANT:** The Fan Profile is NOT the same as your Steelers Account Manager email and password. Set up a new fan profile or tap "Skip."  
5\. Once inside the app, to access your tickets tap "Tickets" in the bottom navigation
