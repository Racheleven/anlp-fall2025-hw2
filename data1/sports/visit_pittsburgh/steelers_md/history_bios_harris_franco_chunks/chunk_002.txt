Franco Harris was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 1990. Harris was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
Harris will forever be remembered for the greatest play not just in Steelers' history, but one recognized by many as the greatest play ever in NFL history, the "Immaculate Reception."
