# Greg Lloyd | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

stood out because he made just about every tackle, all over the field, and the Steelers made him their sixth-round draft pick in 1987. Then, on his first play during his first minicamp, Lloyd covered the back out of the backfield and made the interception. A three-time All-Pro, Lloyd ranks ninth on the team's all-time sacks list with 53.5, played in five Pro Bowls and was named team MVP twice.
