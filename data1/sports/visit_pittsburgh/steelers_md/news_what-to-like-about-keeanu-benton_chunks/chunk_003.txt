**SECOND ROUND
Keeanu Benton, DL, Wisconsin**  
**WHERE HE'S FROM:** Benton attended Joseph A. Craig High School in Janesville, Wis., where he was named First-Team All-State in football and compiled a career record of 127-8 as a wrestler (he was the state's Division I runner-up as a junior and a senior in the 285-pound division). Benton went on to the University of Wisconsin and became the first athlete from his high school to play Division I sports in college in over 30 years, according to draft analyst Dane Brugler of The Athletic.
