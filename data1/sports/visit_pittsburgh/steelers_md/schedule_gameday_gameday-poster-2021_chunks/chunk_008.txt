**Artist:** Abbie Adams  
**Bio:** Abbie Adams is a Pittsburgh-based artist and designer. Adams works with ink and gouache, as well as digital illustration and collage. The simplicity and detail of her imagery define her signature aesthetic and approach, whether working on a mural or a house portrait. Abbie Adams Studio has collaborated with a variety of businesses to create distinctive, locally-themed products and projects. Her love of architecture, repetition, and subtle imperfection influences almost everything she creates. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)
