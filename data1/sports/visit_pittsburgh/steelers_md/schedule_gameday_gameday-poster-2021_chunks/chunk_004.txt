**Bio:** Rick Bach is an American painter, sculptor and graphic designer. Originally from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and a graduate from the Pittsburgh Art Institute, his style was influenced by his early days working alongside his father in an auto body shop. His work ranges from architectural to figurative and is usually painted on metal canvases or sculpted from steel. His motivation is to entertain and provoke while pushing the boundaries between conventional and absurd, functional and useless. Bach currently resides in Washington, D.C., and maintains a studio there as well as in Pittsburgh. For more than 20 years, he has been commissioned by the Big Burrito Restaurant Group to create site-specific murals and sculptures for their restaurants throughout the east coast. Bach's work is in private collections around the world, and he has been featured in numerous solo and group exhibitions. The artist is represented by Zynka Gallery in Pittsburgh and Studio A Global in Washington,
