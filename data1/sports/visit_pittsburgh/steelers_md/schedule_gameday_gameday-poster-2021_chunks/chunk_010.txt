**Artist:** Mike Pfaltzgraff  
**Bio:** My name is Michael Pfaltzgraff. I am a Pittsburgh-based graphic designer. I've been a designer for over 25 years. Growing up in the Pacific Northwest, I had an interest in bikes at a young age. This had a significant influence on my interest in design. I got an early start designing for the bike industry when I was still in high school. Since then, I've been a designer by trade and have worked with some of the most established outdoor companies in the world. There are over 3 million bikes out in the world with my design work on them. For the last ten years I've been running a small, independent design agency based in Pittsburgh. From The North Face to the Carnegie Museum of Natural History, I have worked with a variety of brands on projects that are creatively fulfilling.
