**Artist:** Strawberry Luna  
**Bio:** Starting as a hobby, Strawberryluna is the wife and husband design team of Allison Glancey and Craig Seder. Based in Pittsburgh, they became a creative team in 2005 and quickly went from designing rock posters for bands you 've never heard of (and some of you never will), to designing, illustrating, and creating visuals for some of the largest brands (and bands) in the world. From designs for bands like The National and Belle & Sebastian to working for The TODAY Show to Three Rivers Arts Festival, Strawberryluna loves creating strong, eye-catching designs. Getting to design today's Game Day Poster is a dream come true for these two lifelong Steelers fans. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)
