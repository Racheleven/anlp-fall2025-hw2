# Pittsburgh Steelers Gameday Poster | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

Week 9: Steelers vs. Bears  
**Artist:** Nina Zivkovic  
**Bio:** Nina Zivkovic is an art director, designer, and illustrator at a South Side studio called ocreations. She also freelances to musicians, actors, and entertainment professionals. From national sports nutrition companies to local restaurants and staples, Nina has a vase variety of design & illustrator industry experience, citing entertainment as her favorite: "Illustration and design are like being an actor. You study and research and express yourself through a focused lens, to capture the right emotions and theme. With entertainment illustration clients, you get to get super weird and creative, and with graphic design at ocreations you have rules, goals, and structure. Two different types of challenges, both of which I love." She is continually inspired by art nouveau, comic books, and movie posters, and is an avid football and all things geek-nerd fan.
