Bill Dudley was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 1966. Dudley was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
Before Jerome Bettis, before Franco Harris, and before John Henry Johnson, there was a man who dominated in the backfield for the Pittsburgh Steelers and that was Dudley.
