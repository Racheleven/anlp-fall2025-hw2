Mike Webster was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 1997. Webster was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
When you talk about the center position for the Pittsburgh Steelers, the conversation always starts with Webster. A fifth-round draft pick by the team in the 1974 NFL Draft, Webster anchored the Steelers line throughout his career.
