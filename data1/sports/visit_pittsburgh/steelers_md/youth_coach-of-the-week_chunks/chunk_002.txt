##  Pittsburgh Steelers High School Coach of the Week  
During each high school football season, the Pittsburgh Steelers select a High School Coach of the Week.Â This coach is selected by the Steelers as someone who has a big victory, a turnaround season, does extraordinary work in their community, and/or gives great effort to make a lasting positive impact in his student athletes.Â The Coach of the Week winners are invited to be honored at a Steelers home game and receive a framed certificate signed by NFL Commissioner Roger Goodell and Head Coach Mike Tomlin; a Steelers Coach of the Week cap; and a $1,000 donation to their football program.  
The Coach of the Year is selected following the high school football season and receives a $2,000 donation to his football program.
