# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 2 vs Buccaneers

The defense took care of business, getting the ball back in the hands of Thomspon and company. Kaleb Johnson had his best run of the preseason with a 14-yard gain off right guard, and on fourth-and-one, Thompson hit Connor Heyward for a one-yard gain to move the chains. Kaleb Johnson pulled in a nine-yard reception, and Thompson connected with Miller for 23 yards. A short completion to JJ Galbreath for five yards gave the Steelers the ball at the 13-yard line. Things took a turn, though, when Thompson's pass, intended for Lance McCutheon, was intercepted by Kindle Vildor at the Tampa seven-yard line.  
Both teams exchanged possessions before the half came to an end, sending the Bucs into the locker room up, 14-7.
