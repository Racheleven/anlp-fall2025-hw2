# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 2 vs Buccaneers

The Steelers offense came to life when Rudolph went deep to Wilson for a 42-yard completion to the Tampa 21-yard line. A pass interference call on the Bucs gave the Steelers the ball on the two-yard line. Rudolph hit a wide open Brandon Johnson in the end zone for a two-yard touchdown. Rookie kicker Ben Sauls, with defensive lineman Logan Lee snapping, hit the extra point, cutting the lead in half, 14-7.  
Kyle Trask came in at quarterback for the Bucs and didn't stand a chance when rookie No. 1 pick Derrick Harmon sacked him for an 11-yard loss, forcing a punt.  
"Really just playing within the defense, trying to do my best to help the team out to the best of my ability," said Harmon about the sack. "First sack as a rookie, felt good. It's the preseason so just ready to get that one in the regular season. That's it.
