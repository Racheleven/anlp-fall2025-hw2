# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 2 vs Buccaneers

"Week one was kind of rough for me. I took a big...not a big jump, but just more trusted my ability in the second game. Still a lot of work to do in the back end. Still a lot of work as far as me individually and the team, so just ready to get back to the work."  
The Steelers also made a change at quarterback, with Skylar Thompson stepping in behind center with 29 seconds to play in the first quarter. Thompson immediately went to work, hitting Wilson for a 30-yard gain to the 49-yard line. Thompson came right back with a 15-yard completion to Scotty Miller, who ran out of bounds at the Tampa 39-yard line. That would be it for the drive, though. After an incompletion to Wilson and a one-yard run by Kaleb Johnson, Thompson was sacked for a seven-yard loss, forcing a punt.
