# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 2 vs Buccaneers

Both teams exchanged possessions before the half came to an end, sending the Bucs into the locker room up, 14-7.  
Thompson remained at quarterback to start the second half, completing a 12-yard pass to Gailbreath to get things rolling. After a 10-yard penalty backed them up, Thompson scrambled for a six-yard gain. An incompletion, and a four-yard completion to Trey Sermon weren't enough, forcing a punt.  
The third quarter didn't have a lot of action, until Max Hurleman recovered a muffed punt return by the Bus at the Tampa 21-yard line. Kaleb Johnson rushed for nine yards, followed by an eight-yard run. Sermon took it into the end zone on a two-yard run, tying the game, 14-14 with 3:30 to play in the quarter.
