# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 2 vs Buccaneers

The Bucs drive would stall, with the Steelers forcing them to attempt a 43-yard field goal by Chase McLaughlin. On the field goal attempt, D'Shawn Jamison was called for roughing the kicker, giving the Bucs the ball at the 12-yard line and new life. The Bucs made the Steelers pay when Bridgewater hit Irving in the end zone for a 15-yard touchdown and 7-0 lead, on a drive that was eight plays, 48 yards and took 2:36.  
On the ensuing drive, the Steelers were forced to go three-and-out, when Rudolph's scramble on third down came up one yard short.
