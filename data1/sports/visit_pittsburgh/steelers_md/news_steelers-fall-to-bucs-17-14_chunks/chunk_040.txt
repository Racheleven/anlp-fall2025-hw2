The Steelers kicked off to start the game, with Chris Boswell seeing his first action of the preseason, booting it 63 yards. The Bucs started at the 25-yard line, and Bucky Irving was stuffed for a one-yard loss by Brandin Echols. Irving managed just one yard on the next carry, before he was met by linebacker Cole Holcomb. Bucs quarterback Teddy Bridgewater escaped the pressure from linebacker Nick Herbig and hit Sterling Shepard for a seven-yard gain, before he was taken down by Echols to force a punt.
