# Pittsburgh Steelers Nike Next Ones | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
#### The Nike Next Ones program honors the best high school football players from around the country with an invitation to compete in a skills challenge and experience behind-the-scenes action. The collaborative event, which takes place the week of the Super Bowl each year, allows the nominated players to learn from NFL legends and mentors.  
MATT SIEG - 2024  
We are proud to congratulate Matt Sieg, QB Class of 2026 from Fort Cherry Football! Matt will represent the Steelers at Nike's 'The Next Ones' in New Orleans, an elite showcase featuring 33 of the top high school football athletes during Super Bowl Week.  
Matt has committed to Penn State University to continue his academic and athletic career.
