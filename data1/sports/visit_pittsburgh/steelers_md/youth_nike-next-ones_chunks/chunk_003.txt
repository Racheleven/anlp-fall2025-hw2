# Pittsburgh Steelers Nike Next Ones | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

TIQWAI HAYES - 2023  
Tiqwai Hayes, RB Class of 2025 from the Aliquippa Quips Pittsburgh, PA represented the Steelers at Nike's 'The Next Ones' in Las Vegas during the week of Super Bowl LVIII.  
Tiqwai has committed to Penn State University to continue his academic and athletic career.
