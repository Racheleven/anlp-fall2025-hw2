# Pittsburgh Steelers Game Day Activities | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com  
**FEDEX AIR & GROUND ZONE**  
Located outside of Gate A, the new **44 X 25-yard Football Field** provides a real football feel for all ages, housing the FedEx Air & Ground Zone with various football drills and activities.  
**ART ROONEY AVENUE**  
Art Rooney Avenue is the hub for all things Steelers pregame. Come join us at this interactive experience for fans of all ages.  
**Photo Opportunities -** Take selfies with our Steelers jersey display or compare your size with current & former players!

* Hang out with Steely McBeam
