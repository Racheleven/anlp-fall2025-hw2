## **18\. International Users**

We are required to confirm your identity prior to responding to your ARCO request. Therefore, your ARCO request must include and attach the following: (i) your name, address, or email address to receive an answer, (ii) documents to confirm your identity or the identity of your legal representative (if applicable); (iii) an accurate description of the specific personal data in question (except when access right request); (iv) a description of the ARCO right you are seeking to exercise, or a description of what you are requesting and (v) any other information or document which may facilitate the identification of your personal data; (vi) in case you submit a rectification request, please indicate the modifications to be made and submit the documentation supporting your request.
