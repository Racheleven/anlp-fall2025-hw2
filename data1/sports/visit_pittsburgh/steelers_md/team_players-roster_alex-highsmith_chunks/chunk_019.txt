## Biography

* Reached a career high in special-teams tackles with three in Week 6 vs. Cleveland
* Recorded the first sack of his career in Week 9 at Dallas
* Entered the starting lineup in Week 13 vs. Washington
* Intercepted his first career pass in Week 8 at Baltimore  
**COLLEGE**  
* Four-year letterwinner at Charlotte (2015-19)
* Owns school records for sacks in a game (4.5), season (14.0) and career (20.0) and tackles for loss in a game (5.0) and season (21.5)
* Ranks second in school history with 46.0 tackles for loss and fifth with 185 career tackles
* 2020 NFL Combine participant
* Named to the 2020 NFL All-Combine Team as an edge rusher
* 2020 East-West Shrine Bowl
* 2019 Third Team All-America by both AP and Pro Football Focus
* First 49er to earn all-America honors
* 2020 East-West Shrine Bowl
* 2018 and 2019 First Team All-Conference USA (coaches)
* First 49er to be named first-team All-C-USA twice
* 2019 First Team All-C-USA by PFF and Phil Steele
