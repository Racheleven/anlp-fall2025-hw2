# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 3 at Panthers

After being hurt by penalties, the Steelers benefited from one when Lathan Ransom was called for a 13-yard pass interference penalty on a pass attempt to Freiermuth. Kenneth Gainwell took it five yards to the Steelers 43-yard line, but a 24-yard gain on the ensuing play was negated by a holding call on Freiermuth. On second-and-13, Rudolph found Lance McCutcheon for 15 yards to move the chains to midfield. The offense was stopped there. Warren was hit for a one-yard loss, and Rudolph's third down pass to Freiermuth only accounted for five yards, forcing a punt.
