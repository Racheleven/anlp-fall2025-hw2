# GAME PHOTOS: Preseason Week 3 at Panthers

Kaleb Johnson took the opening kickoff 22 yards to the Steelers 30-yard line, but a penalty on Quindell Johnson pushed them back 10 yards to the 20-yard line to start the game.  
Rudolph started for the third straight week, but this time he had several new weapons, with running back Jaylen Warren and tight end Pat Freiermuth playing for the first time in the preseason. On the first offensive play, center Zach Frazier was called for holding, setting the Steelers up with a first-and-20 at the 10-yard line. Rudolph was under pressure, scrambling for only a three-yard gain. Rudolph hit Warren over the middle for a 12-yard gain, but he fumbled, and Roman Wilson was able to jump on the ball to maintain possession.
