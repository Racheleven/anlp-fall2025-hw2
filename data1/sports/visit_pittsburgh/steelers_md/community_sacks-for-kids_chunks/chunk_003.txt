## **Sacks for Kids, Presented by Bordas & Bordas**  
**For every sack that our defense records this season, the law firm of Bordas & Bordas will donate $500 to the Ronald McDonald House Charities of Pittsburgh and Morgantown. Since 2015, the Sacks for Kids initiative has raised over $301,000.**  
### **2025 Season**  
### **2024 Recap**  
At the home finale on January 4, a representative from Bordas & Bordas (top left) presented a check to Eleanor Reigel (top middle), CEO of RMHC Pittsburgh & Morgantown, alongside families staying at the House. The check represented the contributions raised during the 2024 season.  
### **2023 Recap**  
Eleanor Reigel, the CEO of RMHC Pittsburgh, and families staying at the House are presented with a check at the home finale, thanks to the contributions from Bordas & Bordas.
