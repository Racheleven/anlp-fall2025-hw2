ABOUT THE BENEFICIARY:  
Artists Image Resource (AIR) is a laboratory designed to serve artists, educators, and the community. AIR creates an exciting and dynamic learning environment that fosters experimentation, individuality, and peer mentorship by bringing established and emerging artists together in its print studio and exhibition space on Pittsburgh's Northside. AIR's primary activities are to initiate and facilitate projects with professional artists and create spaces where artists, students and the public interact.
