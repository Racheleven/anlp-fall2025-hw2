# John Henry Johnson | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

Johnson was the Steelers second pick in the 1953 NFL Draft, but instead of joining the Steelers immediately, he went to the Canadian Football League because the money was better. He was with the CFL for just one year, playing for the Calgary Stampeders, before signing with the San Francisco 49ers, spending three seasons there. Johnson played in what was dubbed the 'Million Dollar Backfield' with the 49ers, along with Joe Perry, Hugh McElhenny and quarterback Y.A. Tittle.  
Johnson was traded to the Detroit Lions in 1957, and then acquired by the Steelers via trade in 1960, where he would spend the most productive six seasons of his career.  
Johnson carried the ball 118 times for 621 yards and two touchdowns in 1960, and added 213 carries for 787 yards and six touchdowns his second season.
