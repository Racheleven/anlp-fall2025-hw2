# Sixth-round cornerback brings size and versatility

WHAT HE'S DONE: Lee, 6-foot-3 and 208 pounds, played in seven games as a freshman in 2020 and tied for the Buckeyes' team lead with two interceptions in 2021. He earned Honorable Mention All-Big XII honors after transferring to Texas in 2022. Lee emphasized his experience playing a variety of special teams roles in all four of his collegiate seasons and considers himself position-flexible enough to play cornerback, nickel cornerback or safety in the NFL. His impressive testing at the NFL Scouting Combine in Indianapolis included a vertical leap of 40.5," a broad jump of 10'5" and a 4.53-second 40-yard dash.
