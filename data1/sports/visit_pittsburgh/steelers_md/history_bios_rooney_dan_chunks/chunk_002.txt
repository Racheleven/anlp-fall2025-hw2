Daniel M. Rooney was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 2000. Rooney was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
There aren't many Pro Football Hall of Famers that are inducted one day, and then back to work for what they were inducted for the next day. But there also aren't many Hall of Famers like Rooney.  
Rooney was one of the NFL's most influential owners and a pivotal figure in the growth and development of the league.
