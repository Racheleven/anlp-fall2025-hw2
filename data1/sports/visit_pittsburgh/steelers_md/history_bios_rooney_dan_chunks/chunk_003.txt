# Daniel M. Rooney | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

Rooney was one of the NFL's most influential owners and a pivotal figure in the growth and development of the league.  
Throughout the years Rooney was been a member of the board of directors for the NFL Trust Fund, NFL Films, and the Scheduling Committee. He was appointed Chairman of the League's Expansion Committee in 1973, eventually adding Seattle and Tampa Bay to the NFL in 1976.
