Hall of Honor Museum  
The Steelers Hall of Honor Museum provides an opportunity for fans to learn about the storied franchise and relive the biggest moments in team history. Be one of the first to explore the newest additions, including a rotating exhibit highlight the 50th anniversary of Super Bowl IX (featuring the Lombardi Trophy) and a Hope Harvey display paying homage to the team founded by Art Rooney.

Hall of Honor Class of 2025  
The Hall of Honor Class of 2025 includes three individuals who were part of Super Bowl seasons for the Steelers, with two of them part of the same championship team. Center Maurkice Pouncey, linebacker Joey Porter and quarterback Ben Roethlisberger highlight a class that brought plenty of glory to the black and gold.
