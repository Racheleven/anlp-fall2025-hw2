# What to like about: Kenny Pickett

"He had the most consistent tape this year. It all came together for him in his final season at Pitt. He was healthy, I thought the accuracy was outstanding and I thought the play-making went to another level this year." _ NFL Network analyst Daniel Jeremiah.  
"I know how to win, that's the No. 1 thing in this game." _ Pickett, speaking to the NFL Network at the Senior Bowl.  
**WHERE HE FITS:** That's to be determined now that Pickett, who was considered by many draft analysts to be the most NFL-ready QB in this year's draft class, has joined a quarterback's room that includes Mitch Trubisky and Mason Rudolph. "We're going to have three young quarterbacks with different degrees of experience," Steelers General Manager Kevin Colbert assessed. "Kenny, he'll be a rookie but he will be closer to being ready. Coach (head coach Mike Tomlin) will let that happen naturally. It's just a great option. It was a plus for (Pickett) to be coming in that ready-made mode."
