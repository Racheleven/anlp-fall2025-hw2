# Making an impact by giving back

The Education Law Center's mission is to ensure access to quality public education for all children in Pennsylvania by focusing on the most underserved students: children living in poverty, children of color, children with disabilities, children in the foster care and juvenile justice systems, children experiencing homelessness, English learners, LGBTQ and gender-nonconforming students â and many who are at the intersection of these identities.  
"The Education Law Center appreciates the generous support of the Pittsburgh Steelers," said Hetal Dhagat, Senior Attorney of the Education Law Center. "These funds will help us advocate for education justice to ensure that all children in Pennsylvania have access to quality public education."
