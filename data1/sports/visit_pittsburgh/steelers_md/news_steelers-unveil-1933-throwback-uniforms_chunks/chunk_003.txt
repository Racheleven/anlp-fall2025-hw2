# Steelers unveil 1933 Throwback Uniforms

The uniform pays tribute not just to the 1933 team and where it all began for the Steelers, but also to the City of Pittsburgh and the rich legacy it holds.  
"We're excited to unveil our new throwback uniforms which not only pay tribute to the origins of the Steelers franchise but also to the deep connection we have to our hometown of Pittsburgh," said Steelers President Art Rooney II. "It's especially fitting that we'll debut these uniforms on Sunday night, October 26 when we play the Green Bay Packers â another franchise in the league with a long and rich history."  
The team will wear a version of the 1933 uniform, with some slight adjustments, during the Week 8 matchup against the Packers.
