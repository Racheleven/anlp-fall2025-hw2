Kevin Greene was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 2016 and enshrined on Aug. 6, 2016 after waiting 12 years from the time he retired from the NFL for the honor. Greene was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.
