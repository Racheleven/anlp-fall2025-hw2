Tunch Ilkin always jokes that the title of the book chronicling his career with the Pittsburgh Steelers would be: "Too Late for the Super Bowls; Too Early for Free Agency." Drafted on the sixth round in 1980 as a center from Indiana State, Ilkin became a starting tackle who played both sides of the line of scrimmage during his 13 years with the Steelers. Ilkin played in consecutive Pro Bowls â 1989 and 1990 â and also was an active member of the NFLPA when it negotiated the salary cap/free agency system still in place today. Reggie White might have finished his Hall of Fame career with 198 career NFL sacks, but he never got one against Tunch Ilkin.
