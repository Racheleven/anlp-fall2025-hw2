Terry Bradshaw was voted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame as a member of the Class of 1989. Bradshaw was also an inaugural member of the Steelers Hall of Honor as a member of the Class of 2017.  
Bradshaw was the No. 1 overall pick in the 1970 NFL Draft and he definitely lived up to the hype. He is one of only 13 overall first picks to be inducted into the Pro Football Hall of Fame, and he definitely earned his Gold Jacket.
