#### Design  
The facility was designed by Hellmuth, Obata and Kassabaum (HOK), Inc. out of
Kansas City, MO. The design and construction management team consisted of a
joint venture between Pittsburgh-based Dick Corporation and Barton Malow with
offices in Baltimore, MD. The Architect of Record was L.D. Astorino &
Associates of Pittsburgh.
