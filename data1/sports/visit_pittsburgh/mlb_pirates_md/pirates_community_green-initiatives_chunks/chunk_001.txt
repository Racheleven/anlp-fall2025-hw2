# LET'S GO BUCS. LET'S GO GREEN.  
PRESENTED BY WM  
Since 2009, Pirates Chairman Bob Nutting and the Pittsburgh Pirates have shown
their dedication to sustainability by making strides in the areas of waste
diversion, energy and water conservation, and environmental awareness. From
achieving an 84% diversion rate to decreasing water and energy usage by nearly
20%, the efforts put forth by the Pirates have a positive impact on the
Pittsburgh Community. These achievements would not be possible without the
support of our dedicated partners, whose collaboration and commitment are
instrumental in making the Pirates award winning **Let’s Go Bucs. Let’s Go
Green**. sustainability program a success.  
PARTNERS  
* **412 Food Rescue** – Local food donation partner collaborating with the Pirates, Aramark, and Pirates Clubhouse Executive Chef Tony Palatucci. The Pittsburgh-based nonprofit redirects unused food to those in the community who are experiencing food insecurity.
