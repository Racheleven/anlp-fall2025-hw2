# LET'S GO BUCS. LET'S GO GREEN.

LEAGUE RECOGNITION  
In 2008, Major League Baseball introduced the Green Glove Award to recognize
the team with the highest recycling rate and best practices in diverting waste
from landfills.  
* The Pittsburgh Pirates are proud recipients and nominees for Green Glove Award, showcasing a long-standing commitment to sustainability.
* Since 2016, the Pirates have achieved the highest diversion rate in the National League Central, reflecting consistent leadership in environmental responsibility.  
PNC PARK PATIO GARDEN  
When the sun comes out and the sound of cracking bats fills PNC Park, the
Pirates continue to re-open the PNC Park Patio Garden. The Patio Garden uses
sustainable practices and provides vegetables and herbs, grown on-site, to be
used in Pirates players' favorite healthy dishes prepared by Pirates Executive
Chef Tony Palatucci.  
Since 2022 over 335 pounds of produce was harvested from the Patio Garden.
