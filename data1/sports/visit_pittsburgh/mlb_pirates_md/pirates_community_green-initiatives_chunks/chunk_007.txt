# LET'S GO BUCS. LET'S GO GREEN.

by the Pirates Executive Chef Tony Palatucci.  
RECYCLING  
Just like families at home, our Pirates family works hard each season to
reduce, reuse and recycle, not just on Earth Day, but every day. In over a
decade of sustainability efforts at PNC Park, the Pirates and our partners
have recycled...  
* * *  
Cardboard  
**1,936 Tons**  
That’s the equivalent weight of over 200 T-Rex dinos!  
Fryer Oil  
**259 Tons**  
This oil is recycled and eventually used as biofuel.  
Other Recyclables  
**4,895 Tons**  
Including electronics, toner cartridges, metal, batteries, and more.  
Plastic  
**851 Tons**  
Including 1.3 million plastic bottles used by fans each season.  
Mixed Paper  
**214 Tons**  
Plus, all paper in the front office is at least 30% post-consumer material.  
Aluminum  
**563 Tons**  
Since we have started recycling, we have recycled 563 tons of aluminum.
