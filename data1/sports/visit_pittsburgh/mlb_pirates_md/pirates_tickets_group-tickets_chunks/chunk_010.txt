accordion-contract-145543accordion-expand-145544What time do the gates open at
PNC Park?  
Gates will open one and one-half (1 ½) hours before game time for all start
times excluding 6:40 p.m. games where gates will only open one (1) hour before
game time Monday-Thursday and one and one-half (1 ½) hours before game time
Friday-Saturday. The Home Plate Club will open two (2) hours prior to game
time.
