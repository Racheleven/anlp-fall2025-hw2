PITTSBURGH – Pirates center fielder Oneil Cruz was removed in the 11th inning
of the club’s 3-2 loss to the Braves on Saturday at PNC Park due to lower back
tightness. Cruz appeared to tweak something while running to second base and
slowing up after Tommy Pham drew a walk
