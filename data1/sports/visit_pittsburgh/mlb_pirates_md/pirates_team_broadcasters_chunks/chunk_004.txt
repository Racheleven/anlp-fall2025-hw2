The 47-year-old Block has called baseball for 21 seasons, previously with the

He and his wife, Bethany, a nurse practitioner and South Park native, are

in the South Hills area.  
They reside in Bethel Park with daughters Nancy (9) and Mary Claire (7) and
son Zeke (4).
