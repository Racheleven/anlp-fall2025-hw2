# PIRATES BROADCASTERS  
### Greg Brown  
Greg Brown is in his 32nd year as a radio and television play-by-play
announcer with the Pirates. Only Lanny Frattare (33 years) has called more
games as a play-by-play announcer than Brown, who has described the action of
over 4,000 games in his career behind the mic.  
Known for his outgoing personality and passion for the ballclub, the Emmy
award-winning broadcaster has made the phrase “Raise The Jolly Roger”
synonymous with a Pirates victory and “Clear The Deck, Cannonball Coming” his
signature home run call.
