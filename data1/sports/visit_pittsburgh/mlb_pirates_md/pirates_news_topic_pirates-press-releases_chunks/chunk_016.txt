The Pittsburgh Pirates today presented the final plans for the installation of
commemorative bronze plaques at PNC Park, adorning the messages originally
displayed at the ballpark as part of the “Bucco Bricks” program. The
presentation to the Sports Exhibition Authority (SEA) of Pittsburgh was given
by Pirates President Travis Williams
