The Pittsburgh Pirates today acquired left-handed pitcher Evan Sisk and first
baseman Callan Moss from Kansas City for left-handed pitcher Bailey Falter.
The 28-year-old Sisk made five relief appearances for Kansas City this season,
posting a 1.69 ERA (5.1ip/1er) and 11 strikeouts. He also had four saves, a
3.77 ERA
