### Parking  
The Pirates strongly encourage fans, especially families, to arrive as early
as possible or find alternate parking in the Golden Triangle in one of the
nine Pittsburgh Parking Authority Garages and take advantage of the free "T"
service. Prepaid parking passes will be digital and available on the MLB
Ballpark app. Please have the app downloaded for quick entry.  
Learn More  
### Ballpark Entry  
Gates will open one and one-half (1 ½) hours for all start times excluding
6:40 p.m. games where gates will only open one (1) hour before game time
Monday-Thursday and one and one-half (1 ½) hours Friday-Saturday. The Home
Plate Club will open two (2) hours prior to game time.
