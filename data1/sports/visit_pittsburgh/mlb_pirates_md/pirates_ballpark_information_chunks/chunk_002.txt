#### Ticket Office Hours:  
The Pirates Main Ticket Office is open Monday through Friday from 9:00 a.m. to
5:00 p.m. (or 15 minutes after game ends on gameday). On gameday Saturdays and
Sundays, the Pirates Main Ticket Office will open at 9 a.m. and close 15
minutes after the game ends.
