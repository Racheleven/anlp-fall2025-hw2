Cal Raleigh of the Seattle Mariners has been selected the 2025 Baseball
Digest/Inside Edge MLB Player of the Year. Paul Skenes of the Pittsburgh
Pirates has been named MLB Pitcher of the Year while Aroldis Chapman of the
Boston Red Sox has been voted MLB Relief Pitcher of the Year.
