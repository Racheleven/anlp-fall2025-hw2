Upon further review, the ABS Challenge System has been confirmed for the 2026
MLB season. The Joint Competition Committee voted Tuesday afternoon to bring
the Automated Ball-Strike (ABS) Challenge System, powered by T-Mobile, to the
big leagues following several years of experimentation in the Minor Leagues
and use in MLB
