PITTSBURGH -- The Pirates are making changes to their Major League coaching
staff, parting ways with pitching coach Oscar Marin and third base coach Mike
Rabelo, per source. Additionally, assistant pitching coach Brent Strom will
not have his contract renewed. The Pirates have not yet confirmed the moves.
