#### What should I do if I need to enter PNC Park for the start of the game
and the rest of my group is not here yet?  
You can easily forward tickets to friends via the MLB Ballpark app up to two
hours after the start of that day's game as long as the tickets have not
already been scanned in for entry at PNC Park.  
#### Can I use Apple Wallet for my Pirates tickets?  
Yes. From the bottom of the ticket inside the MLB Ballpark app you can tap the
"Add to Wallet" button to add your ticket to Apple Wallet.  
#### Can I use Google Pay for my Pirates tickets?  
No. Support for adding tickets to Google Pay is not yet available from the MLB
Ballpark app.
