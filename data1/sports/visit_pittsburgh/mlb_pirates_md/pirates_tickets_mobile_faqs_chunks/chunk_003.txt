# MOBILE TICKETING FAQ

To view your tickets:  
1. Open the MLB Ballpark app on your phone
2. Log in to an existing MLB account or create a new account with the same email address used to buy your tickets
3. You may need to verify your email address if this is your first time accessing your tickets in the MLB Ballpark app. You can verify your email address by tapping the Profile icon gear from the top left hand corner of the Home tab, tapping on My MLB Account Emails, where you can have a verification email sent to the email address you used to purchase your tickets.
4. A verification email will be sent to the email address requested. Tap on the link in the email to allow access to your tickets in Ballpark  
That's it! All of your tickets will be available in the Tickets tab’s Wallet
or on the Home tab on the day of the game!
