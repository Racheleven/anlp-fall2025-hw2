#### Which types of tickets will be displayed in the MLB Ballpark app?  
The MLB Ballpark app displays regular season, Postseason, Spring Training and
other event tickets within the Tickets tab’s Wallet. Tickets that you have
forwarded to friends will not show a barcode. There will be a message that the
ticket has been forwarded with a cancel button in place of the barcode.
