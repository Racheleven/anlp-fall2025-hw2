#### What if I never received the Ticket Forward email?  
If you are expecting to receive a ticket forward but were never notified by
email, contact the sender. The sender can cancel the current Ticket Forward
then re-send to you by email or share link. If you or the sender are still
having difficulties, contact Pirates Fans Services at
ticketservices@pirates.com or 1-800-BUY-BUCS.  
#### If the person I forward tickets to deletes the email they received to
accept the ticket(s) before they click "Accept," what should I do?  
The ticket owner should cancel their original ticket forward and then re-
forward the ticket via the MLB Ballpark app so the recipient receives a new
email through which to accept the ticket(s).
