#### Will the MLB Ballpark app feature rewards or exclusive benefits similar
to those featured in other ballparks?  
No. At this time, the MLB Ballpark app for Pirates tickets at PNC Park is used

available in the app. The Pirates may consider other PNC Park-specific app
features in the future.
