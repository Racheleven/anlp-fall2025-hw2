## THE MILLER LITE LANDING  
Located just above the Center Field fenceline, The Miller Lite Landing offers
a fantastic view of the action on the field, as well as the perfect view back
to the Roberto Clemente Bridge and downtown cityscape. The new social
gathering space features new drink rails, tables and high-top seating creating
a relaxed environment for all fans.
