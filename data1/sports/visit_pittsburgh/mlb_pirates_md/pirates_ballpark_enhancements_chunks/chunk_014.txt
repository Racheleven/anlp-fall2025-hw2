## LEFT FIELD LOUNGE

## LEFT FIELD LOUNGE  
The Left Field Lounge exterior is now modified to create one large open
platform with a new picket style drink rail that runs the length of the space.
These enhancements improve the fan experience by allowing groups to
comfortably move about the space, all while allowing for improved sight lines
from within the Left Field Lounge.
