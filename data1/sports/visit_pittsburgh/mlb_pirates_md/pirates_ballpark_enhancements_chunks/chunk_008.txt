## FAT HEAD'S BULLPEN BAR

## FAT HEAD'S BULLPEN BAR  
Replacing the former gameday security building, the creation of this new
gathering space above the center field bullpens includes two rows of drink
rails, comfortable seating, televisions and more. Like all of the new spaces,
the Fat Head's Bullpen Bar offers a great view of the action on the field, so
fans won’t miss a minute of the game while enjoying a craft beer on the
Riverwalk.
