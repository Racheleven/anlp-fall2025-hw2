## THE MARKET  
"As we continue to enhance PNC Park, our goal is to build upon what makes it
so special and provide an exciting and memorable game day experience," said
Pirates President Travis Williams. "The improvements to our main concourse
come after discussions with our fans. These upgrades to improve entry into the
ballpark, flow of the concourse and speed of service are a result of that
feedback."
