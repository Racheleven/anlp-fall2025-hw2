## Deli Dogs and Nachoritas (Left Field)  
Making our way into left field, two of the newer food options immediately made us stop and load up before we got to our seats. **Deli Dogs** (section 135) features upscale takes on the classic ballpark hot dog, with high-quality dogs and buns served Chicago, chili and other styles. **Nachoritas** (section 137) again elevates the simple nachos and cheese we love and adding queso, crema and now even birria to elevate the experience.  
**Jared:** Who knew eating corn at the ballpark would be this good? The elote from Nachoritas might seriously be my favorite food in the ballpark now. The crisp snap of the corn combined with the cotija cheese with a nice kick from the crema, and that's the best bite anywhere.  
**Courtney:** Ok, this Pitt Dog from Deli Dogs is straight 🔥. I'm loving the heat from the cap and the crunch from the coleslaw and fried onions. Perfection.
