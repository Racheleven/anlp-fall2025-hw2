## Something Sweet  
For those of us with a sweet tooth, the cookie sundae is the best dessert option in the park. You can find it at the **Sweet Spot** (section 106) or **The Cookie & Creamery** (section 119) in the infield concourse. Every ice cream/cookie combination goes great with each other, especially during a hot afternoon game, and comes served in a Pirates batting helmet.
