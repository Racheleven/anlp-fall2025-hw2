## New Food Roundup (Throughout PNC Park)  
Looking to try everything new in the park this summer? Me too! Here's a quick recap on where to find everything new this season:  
* Renegade Hot Dog (Section 133 in Pop's Plaza)
* Pitt Dog and Cheddar and Chive Brat (135 at Deli Dogs)
* Roasted corn on the cob, birria beef nachos and caramel churros (137 at LowKey Taco)
* Chicken Biscuit (144 at Coop de Ville)
* Dessert Pierogi (144 at Cannonball Burger)  
**Jared:** My expectations were sky-high after this went viral before the season, and it is absolutely worth the buzz. The pierogis make this visually stunning (it's absolutely a requirement to take photos of every angle before eating) but my favorite elements of this came from the combo of the briny pickles and savory shredded beef and onions. You get some crunch, some snap, some well-developed slow-cooked goodness and a big mouthful of potatoes in each bite - welcome to the pinnacle of ballpark dining.
