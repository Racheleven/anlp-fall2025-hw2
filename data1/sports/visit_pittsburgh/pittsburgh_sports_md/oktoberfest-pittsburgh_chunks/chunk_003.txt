## Oktoberfest Pittsburgh Information  
### Dates  
October 2 - 12, 2025  
### Location  
Oktoberfest Pittsburgh will be held at Fort Duquesne Boulevard, utilizing the westbound roadway from Stanwix Street over to 7th Street and incorporating the 6th Street Bridge.  
### Times  
Sundays - Thursdays, the Biergarten will be open daily from noon to 8 p.m.  
Fridays and Saturdays: noon to 10 p.m.  
### Admission  
General admission to the event is free, though certain activities require the purchase of tickets. Food and drinks will be available for purchase, along with alcoholic beverages for guests 21 and older.
