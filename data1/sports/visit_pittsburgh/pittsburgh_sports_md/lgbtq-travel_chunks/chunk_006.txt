## Dine Out!  
There’s no shortage of fantastic restaurants in Pittsburgh, too. From quick bites in local favorite spots to delicious meals with breathtaking skyline views, you’ve got your choice of delectable picks. Make sure you schedule a stop in East Liberty to check out Square Café, a colorful café known for tasty and creative breakfast, brunch and lunch offerings in a super welcoming space.
