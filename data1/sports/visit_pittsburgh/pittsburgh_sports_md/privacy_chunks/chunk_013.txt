## Who does VisitPittsburgh share private information with?  
VisitPittsburgh may disclose your PII to our member hotels, convention centers, meeting places, and other businesses to permit them to prepare proposals for you in response to your Requests for Proposals and/or provide other requested information. We require our members and any other businesses to whom we disclose your PII to adhere to the restrictions in this Privacy Policy and to use your PII only to provide you with the information you request.  
VisitPittsburgh may disclose PII to certain intermediaries (third party services providers who do not store, retain or use your PII) solely to the extent necessary to provide the information and services you request through VisitPittsburgh.
