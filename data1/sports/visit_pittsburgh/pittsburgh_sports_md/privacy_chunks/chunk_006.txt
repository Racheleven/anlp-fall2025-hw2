## What PII does VisitPittsburgh collect and how?  
When you register on our VisitPittsburgh Web Site, we will ask you for certain PII such as, your address, telephone number, and e-mail address. If you use our Request for Proposal option, we will ask you for detailed information regarding your meeting requirements so that responding proposals from our members are on-target with your needs. If you send VisitPittsburgh correspondence, via email, fax, or regular mail, or if other users or third parties such as businesses send us correspondence about your activities in connection with your use of the Web Site, VisitPittsburgh may retain that information. Further, if any third parties provide information to us about you, we will treat that information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.
