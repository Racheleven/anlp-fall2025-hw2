## What PII does VisitPittsburgh collect and how?

VisitPittsburgh may use cookies, or functionally equivalent technology, to collect certain information about you based upon your behavior while visiting the VisitPittsburgh Web Site. This information may include the Uniform Resource Locator (URL) that you just came from (whether this URL is on the site or not), which URL you next go to (whether this URL is on the site or not), what browser you are using, and your IP address. Certain features of Web Site might only be available through the use of such tools. For example, cookies help to reduce the number of times you need to enter certain information about yourself or a planned event while on the site. Cookies can also help us provide information about services that are targeted to your particular interests. Cookies are stored on your local machine, not on the site. You are always free to decline cookies if your browser has this functionality, although if you do so, you might not be able to use certain features on the VisitPittsburgh
