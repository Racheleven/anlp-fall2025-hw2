## Local Hangouts  
If you’re looking to gather with some of the locals, head into Pittsburgh’s neighborhoods to find a number of eclectic hangouts. With more than 80 bars and restaurants through the South Side flats, Carson Street offers a variety of dining and nightlife from top-shelf whiskey selections at Carmela’s Plates & Pints to a wide selection of beers on tap at the Vault Taproom. Plan a visit to Pittsburgh's North Side, Lawrenceville or Shadyside neighborhoods to explore even more options.
