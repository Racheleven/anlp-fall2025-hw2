## Tips to help you make the most of your campus visit:  
1. Make an appointment - confirm college tour times and interviews.
2. Book your travel in advance - visitpittsburgh.com offers information on hotels, restaurants, events, coupons and more.
3. Allow enough time - spend at least a half day at each school you're interested in attending.
4. Create an itinerary - budget your time and create a plan.
5. Plan something special - leave time for fun! Go to a ball game or on a hike, have dinner Downtown, see a show.
6. Remain objective
7. Interview the school. Relax, be yourself and come up with a good list of questions.
8. Get lost - venture to where the action is. Talk to students. Attend a class. Get a true sense of the personality and offerings of the school.
9. Take good notes and lots of photos
10. Study the region - half of your education will happen outside the classroom and off-campus.
11. Be realistic - seek comfort, not perfection.
