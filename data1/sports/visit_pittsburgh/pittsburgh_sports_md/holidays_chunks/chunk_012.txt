### The Lawrenceville Cookie Tour  
**2025 Dates TBA**  
Experience the annual Lawrenceville Cookie Tour, a beloved holiday tradition! This tour of Lawrenceville features shopping opportunities at local businesses who also offer free cookies to visitors, making it a delicious way to support local shops and celebrate the season. in addition to a sweet treat as you explore Lawrenceville, get your gives wrapped at free gift wrapping stations, purchase your favorite cookies at the community bake sales, meet and take photos with costumed characters and a free shuttle service to get around with all your packages!
