## Downtown Holiday Happenings

### Highmark First Night Pittsburgh  
**New Years Eve! (Dec. 31-Jan. 1)**  
Pittsburgh's signature New Year's celebration, Highmark First Night Pittsburgh! The arts-focused festival will take you across Pittsburgh's Cultural District and features a diverse array of live music, dance, theater, visual arts, and comedy. The festivities culminate with a dazzling parade and the iconic Future of Pittsburgh Ball raising to bring in the New Year.
