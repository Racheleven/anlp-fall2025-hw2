# Insider's Guide: Senator John Heinz History Center  
Explore Pittsburgh’s Heinz History Center, the #1 History Museum in America  
Uncover 250 years of Western Pennsylvania history at the Senator John Heinz History Center, PA's largest history museum. The Smithsonian Affiliate features the original TV set from the iconic _Mister Rogers’ Neighborhood_ , the world's largest collection of Heinz artifacts, and the Western Pennsylvania Sports Museum.
