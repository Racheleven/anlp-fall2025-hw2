## Interactive Exhibits  
With six floors of exciting exhibits and activities, the Heinz History Center is the perfect place to learn from the past while making unforgettable family memories. Explore the _Neighborhood of Make-Believe_ featuring the original set and puppets from _Mister Rogers’ Neighborhood_.  
Design Pop Art, construct bridges, and more in _Discovery Place_ , or slide down the “Liberty Tube” in the museum’s _Kidsburgh_ exhibit. You and your family could even throw a touchdown pass to legendary Steelers and score the winning goal for the Penguins inside the two-floor Western Pennsylvania Sports Museum.  
**Pro Tip:** Don’t forget to punch your _SmartSteps_ stamp card on all six floors of the History Center to receive a FREE Heinz pickle pin!
