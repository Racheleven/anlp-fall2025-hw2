# Best Food at PNC Park
## Primanti Bros.

## Primanti Bros.  
**Section 108**  
You can't go wrong with a **Primanti** 's sandwich piled high with fresh deli meats and cheeses and topped off with French fries and cole slaw. A true Pittsburgh classic. Just around the corner from this booth is Wings & Things by Caliente, another regional favorite selling buckets of fresh wings and boneless wings._  
First-timers read this_ : The Pitts-Burger is the go-to order here, which will be a ground beef patty, coleslaw and fries all piled on freshly-baked white bread.
