## Cinderlands Corner Bar

## Cinderlands Corner Bar  
**Bottom of the left field rotunda**  
Easily the best kept secret in the park right now, but one I'm happy to share. Head to the bottom of the left field rotunda, keep walking around to the back of the structure and grab the best beer in the park direct from Cinderlands._  
Cans Available:_ Raise It, Squish, Lil Cinder Lime, Full Squish  
If these aren't enough for you, check out these unique offerings at PNC Park, or head to a ball game soon for great baseball and a smorgasbord of food choices! What is your favorite food to eat during a Pirates game?
