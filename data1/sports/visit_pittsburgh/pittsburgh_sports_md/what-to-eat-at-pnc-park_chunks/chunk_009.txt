## Manny's BBQ  
**Left Field**  
This newly remodeled barbecue stand is named after former Pirates catcher Manny Sanguillen, who has been known to hang around and sign autographs for fans. Eagle-eyed snack connoisseurs will note the new addition of the topped Mrs. T's mini pierogis you can get with either BBQ pork or chicken _  
On the menu_ : house smoked BBQ pork and chicken, mac n cheese, topped mini pierogis, slaw
