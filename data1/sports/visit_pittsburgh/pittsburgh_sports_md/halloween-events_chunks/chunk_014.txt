## Family Friendly Halloween Activities

### Messfest - Halloween Edition  
**Oct. 25, 2025 from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m.**  
Put on your best costume and head to Kamin Science Center for a frightfully fun day of hands-on science experiments including stirring up colorful slime, launching rockets, and creating giant bubbles. This event is free with general admission.  
### Haunting Halloween Music  
Immerse yourself in the sounds of spooky season by attending one of these concerts:  
* Candlelight: A Haunted Evening of Halloween Classics | Oct. 24 | Be swept up in a haunting performance of popular Halloween hits at Trinity Episcopal Cathedral.
* Tales & Tunes of the BOO-roque | Oct. 25 | Creep into the New Hazlett Theater for spine-tingling music, macabre tales, and eerie images with Chatham Baroque and friends.
