## America's 250th in PA  
Pennsylvania is coming together for its own initiatives across all 67 counties. Giant bells will be created throughout the commonwealth as part of the Bells Across PA project. In total, around 100 bells will be placed by December. Another bell project will see bronze bells placed at sites of historical significance,  
The commonwealth will also be planting a Liberty Tree seedling in every county as part of the celebration. There will also be additional classroom education and an annual volunteer effort on July 20, known as Pennsylvania Day.
