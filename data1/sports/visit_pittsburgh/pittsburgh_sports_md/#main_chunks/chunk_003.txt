### Pittsburgh Steelers  
Pittsburgh is officially "Sixburgh" as the Steelers became the first team in NFL history to win six Super Bowl titles! Steelers Nation spreads far and wide, so grab your Terrible Towel and come celebrate where it all originates: the Home of the World Champion Pittsburgh Steelers. Here we go!  
**Where to See Them:** Acrisure Stadium
**Capacity:** 68,400 (record is 70,622 for the 2022 Backyard Brawl between Pitt and West Virginia)
**Opened:** 2001
**Also Home To:** University of Pittsburgh Panthers D1 football, Rib Fest
**Known****For:** Terrible Towels waving, "Renegade" being played in the fourth quarter, incredible skyline views
**Fun Fact:** This stadium hosted two outdoor hockey games - the 2011 NHL Winter Classic and 2017 NHL Stadium Series.
