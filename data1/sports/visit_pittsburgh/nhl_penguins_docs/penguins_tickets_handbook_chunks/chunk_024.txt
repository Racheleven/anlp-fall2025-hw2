**d. Donation**. Account Holders may donate Tickets to Tickets for Kids Charities which will use the Tickets to provide children in the Pittsburgh community the special opportunity to attend Penguins home games at the Venue. Tickets must be donated at least (72) business hours prior to the Game date. This provides Tickets for Kids Charities sufficient time to find a deserving child and chaperone. To donate, please visit My Penguins Account or call the Penguins Customer Service Department at 412.642.1842. Unfortunately, single seat donations cannot be accepted. To learn more about Tickets for Kids Charities and the community groups it partners with, please visit the Tickets for Kids Charities' website.
