# https://www.nhl.com/penguins/tickets/handbook

It is the Pittsburgh Penguins' standard policy to offer Holders, who are compliant with this Handbook, the opportunity to purchase the corresponding Ticket plan for the playoffs and the following season. Season ticket sales in the future seasons are subject to any changes, modifications, limitations, and/or deadlines that the Pittsburgh Penguins may adopt in their sole discretion (including, without limitation, price increases).  
**2\. ACCOUNT**  
**Ticket Statement.** After submitting an order or establishing a Ticket account (an "Account") with the Penguins, the Account Holder will receive an invoice or written confirmation from the Penguins confirming the number of Tickets that the Account Holder will receive, the Game(s) for which the Holder will receive Tickets, any other benefits purchased by the Account Holder, and a statement of amounts due from the Account Holder (the "Statement").
