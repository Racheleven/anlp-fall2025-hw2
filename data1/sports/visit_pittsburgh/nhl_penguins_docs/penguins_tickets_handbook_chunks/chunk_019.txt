**Account Changes.** Changes to the name of the Account Holder must be submitted to the Penguins in writing and bear the signature of the Account Holder. All requests for name changes should be sent to: Pittsburgh Penguins Customer Service Department, PPG Paints Arena, 1001 Fifth Ave., Pittsburgh, PA 15219, Fax: 412.255.1983. Address or other information changes must be sent to the Pittsburgh Penguins via the Account Holder's e-mail address of record to [email protected]. A fee may be assessed for Account transfers or changes. Only the Account Holder named on the Statement may conduct transactions involving the Tickets. The Penguins retain the exclusive right to settle, in their sole discretion, any and all disputes concerning the ownership of an Account or disputes as to who is the Account Holder.
