**d. Late Payment.** If your Account is not paid in accordance with the payment schedule set forth on your Statement, then the barcodes on the original Tickets may be deactivated, in our sole discretion. To reactivate barcodes, payment is required. We reserve the right to charge a late fee in an amount up to the highest charge allowed by applicable law for the outstanding or late payment of any amount due by Account Holder. Should an Account fall multiple installments behind, the Penguins reserve the right to accelerate payments or require immediate payment of the entire unpaid balance. Nonpayment may result in the cancellation of your Ticket plan and its associated benefits. Ticket cancellation will not release an Account Holder from the Account Holder's obligation to pay all amounts due. If an Account Holder makes check payments, the Penguins reserve the right to assess a $25 fee if the check is returned due to insufficient funds.
