**b. Cancellation, Modification or Termination of Games.** Account Holders and Ticket Holders acknowledge and agree that the Penguins and/or NHL may cancel, postpone, reschedule or relocate games for any reason (e.g., move a regular season game of the Pittsburgh Penguins from the Venue to an alternate venue for the purposes of playing a League Special Event), as determined in its/their sole discretion, as well as to modify or discontinue, temporarily or permanently, any aspect of the rules, operations and presentations of NHL games and events ("Preemption"). Game opponents, dates and times are subject to change without notice.
