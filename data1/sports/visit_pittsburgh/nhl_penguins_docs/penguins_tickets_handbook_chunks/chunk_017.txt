# https://www.nhl.com/penguins/tickets/handbook

**Account Holder.** Account Holder must be an individual living adult or legal business entity. The ticket holder is the person who actually uses the Ticket to rightfully enter the Venue for the Game ("Ticket Holder"). The Account is the online account designated by the Penguins for the Account Holder to use to manage the Tickets, payments, and other rights and obligations of the Account Holder. The Account Holder of record is listed on the Account as the primary contact. Account Holders are encouraged to keep an active email address on file with the Penguins. Email is often the first method of contact for reaching Account Holders about invoice reminders, Game time changes, season ticket holder-only contests and promotions, surveys and special invitations. To make sure you are receiving all potential Account Holder benefits available to you, click on Subscription Preferences from a previously received email, visit https://www.nhl.com/penguins/multimedia/email or call 412.642.PENS
