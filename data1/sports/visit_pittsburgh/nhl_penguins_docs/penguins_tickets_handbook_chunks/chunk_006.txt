**1\. TICKETS**  
The Pittsburgh Penguins season ticket holder ("Account Holder", "Holder" "you" or "your") acknowledges and agrees that the purchase of Tickets are solely for 2025.26 NHL preseason and regular season Games presented by the Pittsburgh Penguins and played at PPG Paints Arena, and for the avoidance of doubt, shall not include tickets, or the right to purchase tickets, to any other event or game, including, but not limited to, any NHL European Global Series Games, NHL China Games, NHL Winter Classic, NHL Heritage Classic, NHL Stadium Series, NHL All-Star or NHL Draft event ("League Special Event") to the extent held in the Pittsburgh market or hosted by the NHL at PPG Paints Arena or otherwise in which the Pittsburgh Penguins may participate.
