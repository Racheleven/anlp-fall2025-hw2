**Non-Penguins Event Tickets**  
Non-Penguins group tickets to select Venue events can be purchased through the Penguins Ticketing Department at 412.642.PENS (7367). To discuss group discounts, requirements and availability, please call your Season Ticket Account Representative or e-mail [email protected]. If interested in purchasing a suite or loge for a specific non-Penguins event, please contact your Penguins Season Ticket Account Representative.  
Individual tickets to non-hockey events at the Venue can be purchased one of several ways:  
Online at http://www.ticketmaster.com/
DICK'S Sporting Goods Box Office located at PPG Paints Arena's Highmark Gate (credit cards only)
