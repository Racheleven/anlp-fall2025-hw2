**SEASON TICKET AUTOMATIC RENEWAL AND PLAYOFF PRIORITY** You have elected to purchase Tickets to either (1) each and every preseason and regular season game during the 2025.26 season (a "Full Season Ticket Plan") or (2) a specified number of preseason and regular season games for the 2025.26 season not amounting to the whole season (a "Half Season Ticket Plan") (each such plan a "Season Ticket Plan").  
This Agreement governs the terms of your Ticket purchase regardless of the manner in which you have agreed to purchase the Tickets. This Agreement will automatically renew for subsequent seasons unless you opt-out.
