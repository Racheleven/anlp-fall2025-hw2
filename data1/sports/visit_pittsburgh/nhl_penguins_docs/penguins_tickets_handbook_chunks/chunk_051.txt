**Important Contact Information**  
The Pittsburgh Penguins Ticketing and Customer Service Departments have been designed to better assist season ticket holders. Thus, it is our goal to make your experience throughout the year as enjoyable as possible. Should you have any questions or concerns during the season, do not hesitate to contact your Season Ticket Account Representative or the Customer Service Department. The Penguins Ticketing Department is available Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. On weekend game days, the office opens at 10:00 AM. If calling within 2 hours of the game, please contact the game night box office at 412.804.7824.
