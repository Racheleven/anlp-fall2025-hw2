**e. Resale.** Account Holders can visit the NHL Ticket Exchange section of their Account and post their Tickets for sale to other fans. The transaction is entirely automated and processed by Ticketmaster. Penguins' fans who purchase Tickets through NHL TicketExchange receive a mobile Ticket with a new, unique barcode identifying the buyer as the new owner of that Ticket. If your posted Tickets are sold through NHL Ticket Exchange, you have the opportunity to select direct deposit to a bank account or a credit to your Account that may be applied to individual game purchases, playoff payments, or your season ticket Statement. Your use of NHL Ticket Exchange is governed by the NHL Ticket Exchange terms and conditions. To authorize the Penguins to apply your resale credit to a specific game or plan, please contact the Customer Service Department at 412.642.1842 during normal business hours or e-mail [email protected].
