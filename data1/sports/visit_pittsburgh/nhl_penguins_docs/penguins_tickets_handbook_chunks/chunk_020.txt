**a. Right to Purchase Other and Future Tickets**. Account Holders who are in compliance with these Terms and Conditions will have a right of first refusal to purchase tickets (in the same seat location as Tickets for the current regular season) for any NHL Playoff games in which the Penguins compete, if any, that take place at the Venue during the current season for which Account Holder holds Tickets. Season ticket sales for future seasons are subject to any changes, modifications, limitations, and/or deadlines that the Penguins may adopt in their sole discretion (including, without limitation, price increases). The Penguins reserve the right to refuse to sell future season tickets to any individual or entity that fails to comply with these Terms and Conditions or the Handbook. The Penguins or the Venue may, in their sole discretion, offer Account Holders the ability to purchase special priced tickets for other games or events at the Venue.
