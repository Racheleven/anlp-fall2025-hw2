**h. Accessible Seating and Parking.** Accessible seating is located throughout the Venue's lower and upper concourses. Wheelchair accessible seats in the Coca-Cola Zero Sugar (200) Level may be reached by elevators. Account Holders may request accessible or limited mobility tickets for individual games by calling the Penguins Customer Service Department at 412.642.1842. Accessible ticket purchases and exchanges are first-come, first-served. Should special seating be needed, advance notice is encouraged. Accessible parking spaces are located in the Venue's East and South parking lots. You must have a valid handicap person placard to park. Accessible parking is first-come, first-served and spaces are limited. An early arrival is strongly recommended.
