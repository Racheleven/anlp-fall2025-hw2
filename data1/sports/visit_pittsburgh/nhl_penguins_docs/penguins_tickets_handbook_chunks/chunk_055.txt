**Dairy Queen Birthday or Anniversary Requests**  
By making a monetary donation to the Pittsburgh Penguins Foundation, you can surprise someone with a birthday or anniversary greeting at any Penguins home game. A public address announcement will alert guests of the beginning of the display. To have someone's name displayed, please visit pittsburghpenguins.com for details. All requests must be placed at least 48 hours prior to the selected game.
