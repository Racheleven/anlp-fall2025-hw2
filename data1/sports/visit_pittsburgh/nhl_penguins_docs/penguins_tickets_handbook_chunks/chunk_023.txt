# https://www.nhl.com/penguins/tickets/handbook

1. You must know the recipient's email address or mobile phone number.
2. After the invitation is sent, the recipient must build an Account Manager account to accept the Tickets.
3. Once a Ticket is accepted or stored, the original Ticket is voided.
4. At any time, the new Ticket Holder is able to log back into Account Manager and view a mobile version of the Ticket. This mobile Ticket can be used for entry at all gates at PPG Paints Arena.
