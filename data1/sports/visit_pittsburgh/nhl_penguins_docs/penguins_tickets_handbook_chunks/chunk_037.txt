# https://www.nhl.com/penguins/tickets/handbook

**a. Lost Tickets.** The Penguins are not responsible for lost, stolen, misplaced or forgotten Tickets. The Penguins shall not be obligated to admit a Ticket Holder to a Game unless a valid Ticket is presented. We may, in our sole discretion, issue replacement tickets for lost or stolen Tickets, but are under no obligation to do so. If your Tickets are lost, destroyed or inaccessible and you need a replacement Ticket issued, please call the Penguins Customer Service Department at 412.642.1842 or visit the Penguins Ticketing Department at the Venue. Only the Account Holder of record can request a replacement Ticket. Please note: this service is to be used on an emergency-only basis. It should not and will not be used as an alternative to viewing and forwarding mobile tickets via Account Manager.
