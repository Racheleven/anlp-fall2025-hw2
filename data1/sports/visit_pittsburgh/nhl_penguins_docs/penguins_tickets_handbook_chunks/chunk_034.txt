**5\. COSTS AND PAYMENT**  
**a. Ticket Cost.** All Ticket payments are due by the date indicated on the Statement. A third party may make payments on a Ticket account that has been established in the name of another individual or entity. Such payment, however, does not establish any right to the Tickets, Account, associated Tickets, or benefits.  
**b. Method of Payment.** In the event that you elect to pay for your Tickets in multiple installments, you authorize the Pittsburgh Penguins to charge the credit card on file, or any substitute credit card you later provide, for each installment on or after the date it is due.  
**c. Deposit.** We may require Account Holders to submit a non-refundable deposit, as determined in the Penguin's sole discretion, to establish the Account and/or reserve the Tickets.
