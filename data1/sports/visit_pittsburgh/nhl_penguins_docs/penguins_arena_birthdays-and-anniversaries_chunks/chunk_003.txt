# get-in-the-game-header  
## DQ Birthday and Anniversary Greetings  
Wish the Pittsburgh Penguins fan in your life a happy birthday or anniversary with a special greeting during an upcoming home game! For a $100 donation to the Pittsburgh Penguins Foundation, the first and last name of your friend or family member can be displayed!  
The Dairy Queen Birthday and Anniversary Greetings at PPG Paints Arena are shown in the first period on the LED rings.
