## Eat'n Park Chili Goal! 7 Goals!  
Get a free cup of chili at participating Eat'n Park locations if the Penguins score 7 goals at home. Available the following day, starting at 11am. Guests must present mobile ticket in Google or Apple Wallet. One per check, valid for dine-in only with purchase.
