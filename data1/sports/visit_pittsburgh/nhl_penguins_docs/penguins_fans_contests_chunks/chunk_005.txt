## DICK'S Sporting Goods Jr. Starter of the Game  
The DICK'S Sporting Goods Jr. Starter of the Game gets to stand next to members of the Pittsburgh Penguins during the National Anthem. To participate in this contest, parents or guardians of a youth hockey player or goalie between the ages of 8 and 12 should submit a brief essay written by the player that explains how they demonstrate the qualities of a Jr. Starter.
