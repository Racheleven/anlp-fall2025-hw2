## Lady Byng Memorial Trophy  
_Sportsmanship_  
An annual award "to the player adjudged to have exhibited the best type of sportsmanship and gentlemanly conduct combined with a high standard of playing ability."  
* 1981 Rick Kehoe
* 1995 Ron Francis
* 1998 Ron Francis
