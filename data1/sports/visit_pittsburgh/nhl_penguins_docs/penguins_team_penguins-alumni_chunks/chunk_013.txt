## Scholarship Program  
The Pittsburgh Penguins Alumni scholarship is presented annually to a western Pennsylvania high school senior for academic excellence and for providing leadership, sportsmanship, and a positive attitude as a member of their school's hockey program. Students must have a minimum grade point average of 3.2 to qualify for the scholarship. The scholarship, valued at $2,000, is co-sponsored by the NHL Alumni Association.
