## Golf Tournament and Summer Sticks  
In 1998, the Penguins Alumni Association created the Alumni Charity Golf Classic to assist local children's charities. Since its inception, the event has raised over $740,000 for local non-profits.  
In 2018, the alumni merged golf tournaments with the current Penguins team and combined their efforts to raise charitable funds with a new golf-themed event - Summer Sticks - Topgolf Edition. Proceeds from this event benefit the Pittsburgh Penguins Foundation Scholarship program and alumni charities.
