Locker Room Leadership is a five-week program with weekly videos and assignments for students to follow along. Videos contained insight into different aspects of leadership, as well as first-hand stories and advice from local business leaders such as **Amy Smiley from 84 Lumber** , **Ellen Rossi from the EQT Foundation** , and **Chris McComish from S &T Bank**.  
Weekly assignments were submitted online, and the top-ranking assignments each week earned students tickets, autographed merchandise, Locker Room Leadership merch, and more! The top-performing student each week received a custom jersey!
