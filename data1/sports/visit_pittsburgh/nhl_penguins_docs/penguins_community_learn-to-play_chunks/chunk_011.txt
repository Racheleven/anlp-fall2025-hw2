## About Little Penguins

remove add Does my child need to know how to skate before they sign up for Little Penguins?  
It is **strongly** recommended that children complete a "Learn to Skate" session through their local ice rink prior to enrolling in Little Penguins. In addition, most rinks have regular public ice skating sessions which are a great way to gain experience and confidence on the ice. For those looking to register for a "Learn to Skate" program, please contact your local ice rink.
