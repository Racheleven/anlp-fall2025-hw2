## Pittsburgh Penguins Cookie Policy

For more information about cookies, please visit www.allaboutcookies.org.  
**2.WHICH TYPES OF COOKIES DO THE PENGUINS PARTIES USE?**  
The Websites use the following categories of cookies on this:  
* **Strictly Necessary Functionality Cookies** : These cookies are essential to enable you to move around the Websites and use their features (e.g., user authentication). Because these cookies are necessary for the functioning of the website.
* **Other Functionality Cookies** : These cookies allow the Websites to remember the choices that you make (e.g. language or the region you are in) and provide enhanced, more personal features.
* **Performance Cookies** : These cookies collect information about how you use the Websites, e.g. which pages you visit most often, to improve how the Websites work.
