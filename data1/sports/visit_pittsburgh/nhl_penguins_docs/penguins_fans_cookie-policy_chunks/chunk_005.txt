## Pittsburgh Penguins Cookie Policy

* **Performance Cookies** : These cookies collect information about how you use the Websites, e.g. which pages you visit most often, to improve how the Websites work.
* **Targeting/Social Media/Advertising Cookies** : These cookies are used to deliver advertisements considered relevant to you and your interests. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement as well as help measure the effectiveness of the advertising campaigns. They remember that you have visited the Websites and this information may be shared with other organizations, e.g. advertisers. Social media cookies help connect you to your social media networks and share content through social media.  
Please see below a more detailed list of the cookies we use on the Website.  
The Websites use cookies that expire once you close your browser (called session cookies) as well as cookies that stay on your device until you delete them or they expire (called persistent cookies).
