**3.HOW CAN YOU CONTROL COOKIES?**  
Strictly necessary cookies are necessary to provide the Websites to you.  
You may manually delete the cookies on your device or configure the settings of your browser to activate, disable or delete cookies. Detailed instructions are provided by your browser.
