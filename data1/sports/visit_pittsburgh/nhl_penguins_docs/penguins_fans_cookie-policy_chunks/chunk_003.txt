**Cookie Policy**

This cookie policy (the "Cookie Policy") describes how https://www.nhl.com/penguins/ and other websites that link to this Cookie Policy (the "Websites") use cookies and similar technologies. Please note that the Websites are part of NHL.com, and the NHL and its partners may use cookies not described by this Cookie Policy to collect or permit others to collect information on the Websites.  
**1.WHAT ARE COOKIES?**  
The Websites place small data files called cookies on your device accessing the Websites to ensure that the Websites function properly, to track how you use the Websites, to improve the Websites and your user experience, and to tailor the Websites to your interests. The type of Personal Information that each specific cookie on the Websites processes is described in section 4 ("Which specific cookies does the Penguin Parties use?").
