## Season Ticket Payment Benefit  
Penguins Power Play Package card holders will receive a gift, courtesy of First National Bank, when using their Penguins credit or debit card to pay for their Penguins season tickets. Penguins season ticket holders should contact the Penguins Ticketing Department if making a payment with the Penguins credit or debit card. For the 2025.26 season, fans redeeming will receive an authentic Penguins home jersey featuring the F.N.B. patch. The gift may change year over year, but value will be estimated at $200. This offer is active for season ticket purchases and renewals for the 2025.26 season.  
* For questions related to Penguins Power Play Package benefits, including how to redeem, please contact the Penguins customer service team at [email protected]
* For questions related to your First National Bank checking account, call 1-800-555-5455 or visit your nearest FNB branch
