One of the best two-way centers in NHL history, Francis enjoyed his most dominating offensive stretch of hockey with Pittsburgh. Teaming on a line with fellow Hall of Famer Jaromir Jagr, Francis led all NHL players with 407 assists and ranked third with 548 points during the final six years of his Penguins career from 1992-98.
