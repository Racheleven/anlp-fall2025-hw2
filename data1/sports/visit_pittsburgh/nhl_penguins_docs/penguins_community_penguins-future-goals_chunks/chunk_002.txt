Pittsburgh Penguins Future Goals Hockey Scholar is a unique science, technology, engineering and math (STEM) online resource for students in grades 4-8. This online course leverages the exciting game of hockey as a learning vehicle to energize students in these critical topic areas!  
Since 2014, over 129,300 students have participated in the Hockey Scholar, contributing to over 184,705 hours of learning!
