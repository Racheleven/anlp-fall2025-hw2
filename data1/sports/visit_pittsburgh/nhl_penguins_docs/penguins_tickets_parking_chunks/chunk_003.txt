PPG Paints Arena's accessible dropoff is located on Fifth Avenue outside the EQT Gate. Fans with accessible parking placards are encouraged to pre-purchase parking in the Fullerton Lot on Centre Avenue. Accessible spaces are first come, first served.  
PPG Paints Arena's designated rideshare pickup and dropoff zone is located at the corner of Fullerton Street and Wylie Avenue. Please exit the building from the Highmark or F.N.B. Gate.
