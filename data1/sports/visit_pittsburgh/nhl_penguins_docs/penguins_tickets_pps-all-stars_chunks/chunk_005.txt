## **To Access Your Digital Tickets on Game Day:**  
1. **Download** the Penguins App in the App Store or Google Play.
2. Select **Tickets** from the homepage menu.
3. Tap **Manage My Tickets**.
4. **Sign in** with the email address used during the redemption process.
5. Click the **Game Graphic**.
6. Tap **View Barcode.** Barcode will appear 48 hours prior to game time.
