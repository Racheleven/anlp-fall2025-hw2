## Wesco Sustainability Initiatives  
Wesco's sustainability strategy is deeply integrated into its business operations, aiming to drive meaningful environmental, social, and governance (ESG) progress. Wesco has set ambitious 2030 goals, including significant reductions in greenhouse gas emissions, waste diversion from landfills, and workplace safety incidents. Their initiatives span renewable energy projects that potentially avoided more than 53,600 metric tons of CO2 emissions, enhanced fleet efficiency, and contributions to global community causes. Through strong governance, emphasizing diversity and ethics, and fostering employee growth and social impact, Wesco demonstrates a commitment to building a sustainable and inclusive future.
