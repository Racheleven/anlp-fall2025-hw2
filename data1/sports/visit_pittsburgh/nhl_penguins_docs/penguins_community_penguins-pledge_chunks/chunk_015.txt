## Penguins, EQT and Persefoni Team Up to Meet Sustainability Goals  
Thanks to EQT, official Net Zero partner, the Penguins are taking further steps to measure and reduce greenhouse gas emissions at PPG Paints Arena. The Penguins are working with Persefoni leveraging their advanced carbon accounting tools and expert guidance to measure, manage, and effectively reduce its greenhouse gas emissions. Fans can visit the EQT Gate at PPG Paints Arena to follow along.
