Plan Your Trip
--------------  
Maximize your Soul Food Festival experience by exploring everything [Downtown Pittsburgh](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/neighborhoods/downtown-pittsburgh/) has to offer. When [planning your visit](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/plan-your-trip/), consider [booking your stay](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/hotels-resorts/) at one of the many hotels near Market Square to be steps away from the festivities.
