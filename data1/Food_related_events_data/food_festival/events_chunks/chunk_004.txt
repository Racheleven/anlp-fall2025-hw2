### Picklesburgh
**Date & Time:** July 2026 (Dates TBA)
**Description:** For three days in July, Downtown Pittsburgh will transform into Picklesburgh , highlighted by a giant flying pickle balloon. Picklesburgh is an annual festival that celebrates pickling in all its glorious incarnations and goes beyond the dill pickle to include international dishes and handcrafted foods from local chefs; “how-to” demos; a selection of artisan cocktails; pickle themed merchandise, live music, and even a competitive pickle juice drinking contest.
**Link:** https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/pittsburgh-is-picklesburgh/  
### The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival
**Date & Time:** Sept. 6, 2025 | The Stacks at 3 Crossings
**Description:** Make time for the Pittsburgh Taco Festival , an annual food festival that celebrates tacos and the city's Latin-American community.
