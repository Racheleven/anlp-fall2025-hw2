Pittsburgh is a [foodie’s haven](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/restaurants-culinary/), and our [taco scene](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/pittsburgh-taco-spots/) is no exception. With freshly made corn tortilla shells and an abundance of flavorful fillings, this annual taco fest in Pittsburgh is the ultimate culinary adventure through Mexican cuisine. Stroll through an array of top-notch vendors, where the aroma of Mexican spices will beckon you into trying soul-comforting bites.
