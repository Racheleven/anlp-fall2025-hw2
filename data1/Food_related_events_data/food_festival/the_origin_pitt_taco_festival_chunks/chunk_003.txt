Every year, the festival generates support for non-profit organizations that aid Pittsburgh’s Latin American community. Nestled in the [Strip District](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/neighborhoods/strip-district/), authenticity is at the core of The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival, brimming with local food establishments, artisans, and musicians.
