The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival  
Featured Experiences
--------------------  
### Indulge in a Variety of Mouthwatering Eats  
From classic dishes to modern twists, The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival boasts an array of scrumptious offerings. Along with exceptional tacos, this festival sets a vibrant setting that’s perfect for enjoying empanadas, ice pops, pupusas, and arepas. Vegan and gluten-free options will also be available at different vendors. Indulge in decadent delights from eateries and food trucks such as [Tocayo](https://www.tocayopgh.com/), [Mi Empanada](https://miempanada.com/), and [Vaya](https://www.vayatruck.com/). Check the [official website of The Original Pittsburgh Taco Festival](https://www.pghtacofest.com/vendors) for more details about each local business.
