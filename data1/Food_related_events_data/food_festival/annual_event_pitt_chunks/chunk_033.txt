### [Little Italy Days](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/events-festivals/annual-events/bloomfield-little-italy-days/)  
Everyone's Italian for a weekend at this free, four-day festival in [Bloomfield](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/neighborhoods/bloomfield/) featuring authentic Italian-American cuisine, live entertainment, and so much more.
