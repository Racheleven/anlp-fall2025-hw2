### [Pittsburgh Fringe](http://www.pittsburghfringe.org/)  
Spanning venues throughout the city, [Pittsburgh Fringe](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/pittsburgh-fringe-festival/) is a performing arts festival featuring international, national and local artists and includes theater, comedy, visual arts, puppetry, music, opera, spoken word and much more.
