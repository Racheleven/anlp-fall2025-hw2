### [EQT Children's Theater Festival](http://pghkids.trustarts.org/)  
This sprawling festival happens throughout [Downtown Pittsburgh](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/neighborhoods/downtown-pittsburgh/) at theatres, parks, universities and other businesses as children exercise their creativity through live performances, scavenger hunts, brick building, STEM activities and much more. Most activities are free of charge!
