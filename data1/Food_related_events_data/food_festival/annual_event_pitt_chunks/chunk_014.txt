### [Millvale Music Festival](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/events-festivals/annual-events/millvale-music-festival/)  
Voted the Best Music Fest of 2022 and 2023 by Pittsburgh City Paper, this free two-day festival features hundreds of bands at dozens of indoor and outdoor locations all throughout [Millvale](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/insiders-guide-to-millvale/).
