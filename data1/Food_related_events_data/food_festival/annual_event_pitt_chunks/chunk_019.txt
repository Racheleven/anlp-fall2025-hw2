### Juneteenth Freedom Day  
This [free, family-friendly event](http://www.stoptheviolencepgh.com/wpa-juneteenth/) is a celebration of all Cities and Boroughs in Allegheny County and with four days of cultural activities, speakers, live music, a parade and local vendors!
