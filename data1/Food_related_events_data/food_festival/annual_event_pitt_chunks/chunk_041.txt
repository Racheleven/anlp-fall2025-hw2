### [Reel Q Pittsburgh LGBTQ+ Film Festival](http://reelq.org/ "external link")  
The annual Reel Q film festival in October is the sixth oldest LGBTQ+ film festival in the world and the longest-running film festival in Pittsburgh. Reel Q spans 10 days with a variety of [programs, panels, parties and community activities](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/reel-q-pittsburghs-lgbtq-film-festival/). The festival screens approximately 20 locally first-run feature films and 50+ short films spanning multiple genres.
