### [City of Pittsburgh Fourth of July Celebration](http://pittsburghpa.gov/july4/)  
The City of Pittsburgh’s [Independence Day celebration](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/celebrate-the-4th-of-july-in-pittsburgh/) returns to the Point on July 4th! Enjoy a family-style picnic at [historic Point State Park](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/directory/point-state-park/), complete with patriotic music and food vendors from 4 to 10 pm.
