### **What is it?**  
#### Picklesburgh is the destination for all things pickled!  
Voted the #1 Specialty Food Festival in the Country four times by the readers of USA Today, Picklesburgh goes beyond the dill pickle to feature handcrafted food and artisan drinks featuring pickled ingredients from local chefs, restaurants, and more. Plus, don’t miss pickle-themed snacks and merchandise, fun contests, and entertainment set to the backdrop of picturesque Downtown Pittsburgh.
