## PICKLE JUICE DRINKING – Competition Rules

- In the event of a tie in preliminary rounds, all winning contestants will advance to the final championship round on Sunday.
- In the event of a tie in the final round, the tying contestants will share the prize and glory of pickle juice drinking champions. However, if one of the tied contestants would like to challenge for a tie breaker, then a tie breaker round can occur to determine a final winner. Both tied contestants must agree for the tie breaker round to take place. The prize for the winner of the championship round on Sunday, July 13 will be $500 cash and the grand title of ‘Duke of Drinking’.
