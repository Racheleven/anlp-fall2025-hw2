## PICKLE JUICE DRINKING – Competition Rules
- All contestants will be provided with one quart jar of pickle juice (roughly 32 oz of pickle juice).
- Contestants may not touch the jar of juice until the judges say “Go”.
- The contestant who finishes the jar of pickle juice first will be declared the winner of that round. Contestants who noticeably spill, splash or regurgitate the pickle juice will be disqualified, at the discretion of the judges.
- All judges observing will have final decision and say in the event of disqualifications or closely contested rounds.
- In the event of a tie in preliminary rounds, all winning contestants will advance to the final championship round on Sunday.
