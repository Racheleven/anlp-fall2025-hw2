## PICKLE EATING – Competition Rules
- All contestants will be provided with a supply of pickled cucumbers that may be one specific type of pickles (i.e. whole pickles, chips, or spears) or a combination of whole pickles, chips and spears altogether. All contestants will receive the same weight (3 lbs.) of pickles. The contestant who eats the most pickles, by weight, within 4 minutes will be deemed the winner of that round.
- Contestants will be allowed to “chipmunk” and will have an additional 30 seconds after the round is finished to swallow the remaining pickles in their mouth. Any pickles remaining after 30 seconds will have to be spit out.
- The winner will be determined by weight of remaining pickles in the contestants’ bin and by judges appointed by the Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership. The judges’ decisions are final and absolute.
- Contestants who regurgitate, or spit out, the pickles will be disqualified, at the discretion of the judges.
