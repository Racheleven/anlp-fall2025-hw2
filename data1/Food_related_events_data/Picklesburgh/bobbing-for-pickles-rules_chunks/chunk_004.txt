## BOBBING FOR PICKLES – Competition Rules
- All contestants will be provided with a supply of pickled cucumbers that may be one specific type of pickles (i.e. whole pickles, chips, or spears) or a combination of whole pickles, chips and spears altogether. All contestants will receive the same weight of pickles.
- Contestants may only use their mouths to retrieve the maximum number of pickles in the allotted time 90 seconds, which must be deposited into an adjacent receptacle.
- Contestants may not use their hands to touch any of the pickles or they will be immediately disqualified.
- Contestants may touch the sides of their basin and receptacle so long as they do not touch any of the pickles.
- The winner will be determined by who has the highest weight of pickles in the deposit receptacle and by judges appointed by the Pittsburgh Downtown Partnership. The judges’ decisions are final and absolute.
