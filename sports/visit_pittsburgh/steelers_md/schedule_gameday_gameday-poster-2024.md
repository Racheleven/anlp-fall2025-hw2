# Pittsburgh Steelers Gameday Poster | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

**The Pittsburgh Steelers, in collaboration with Commonwealth Press, are releasing 9 gameday posters, one for each home game during the 2024 season, and one to commemorate the entire season! Local artists sourced by Commonwealth Press will design each poster to commemorate the individual game. A limited number of posters will be available for purchase on game day at the Steelers Pro Shop.**

_All gameday poster proceeds will benefit_ **_Artists Image Resource_** _located in Pittsburgh._

**ABOUT THE BENEFICIARY:** Artists Image Resource (AIR) is a laboratory designed to serve artists, educators, and the community. AIR creates an exciting and dynamic learning environment that fosters experimentation, individuality, and peer mentorship by bringing established and emerging artists together in its print studio and exhibition space on Pittsburgh's Northside. AIR's primary activities are to initiate and facilitate projects with professional artists and create spaces where artists, students and the public interact.

**Bio:** Seth Storck is a user experience designer & illustrator living and working in Pittsburgh, PA.

Seth grew up in central PA where everyone he knows roots for the Steelers. He started drawing at a very young age and has never stopped. He moved to Pittsburgh in 2002 to attend the Art Institute to study graphic/web design.

Fun fact: Seth shaved the Steelers logo into the back of his head for the 1995 Super Bowl.

Week 11: Steelers vs. Ravens

**Bio:** Abby Winkler is an illustrator living in Pittsburgh, PA. In 2011, she graduated from Edinboro University of Pennsylvania with a BFA in studio art. Although her background is in painting, she gravitated towards illustration and its ability to use drawing as an educational tool. Her background as a fine artist initiated her love of color and composition, and her experience as a commercial artist sparked an interest in hand-lettering and vintage-style graphics.

Her favorite projects to work on allow her to bring her individual style to help tell someone's story, or to explain the significance of beloved places. Since 2017, she has been steadily working on a personal illustration project called 100 Days of Pittsburgh (#100daysofpittsburgh on Instagram), where she illustrates 100 people, places and landmarks connected to Pittsburgh. This has been invaluable in finding her creative voice and personal style, and has allowed her to work with local clients who have a shared interest.

Abby strives to create illustrations that inform in an approachable and fun way, using joyful color palettes and inventive lettering styles. Her specialties are portraits, hand-lettering, and architecture. Everything is drawn by hand with a pen, usually a Micron fineliner. She adds color digitally in Photoshop, or sometimes applies it traditionally with watercolor or gouache. Also, she likes to scan in textures made by hand to layer within her illustrations in order to further incorporate a handcrafted touch. This marriage of analog and digital techniques is something that keeps her endlessly interested and helps to create the most dynamic images possible.

Week 5: Steelers vs. Cowboys

**Bio:** Brian Dumm is a professional artist and art educator from Ebensburg, Pennsylvania where he works and lives with his wife and two daughters. Brian is represented by Storybook Arts Inc. in New York, and his award-winning illustrations have been featured in over a hundred publications globally since 2010. He has completed artwork for such notable clients as Scholastic, McGraw-Hill, and the Sheetz corporation.

Brian earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from Penn State University in 2000 and a master's degree in Drawing and Painting from Indiana University of Pennsylvania in 2013. His fine-arts drawing/paintings have shown in galleries through Pennsylvania, Ohio, and Maryland.

Brian is a member of the Pittsburgh Society of Illustrators, The Allied Artists of Johnstown, and the Pennsylvania Art Education Association. Brian's work is largely illustrative, having been inspired by his earliest visual passions for comic book art, skateboard graphics, and album covers.

Brian is also an avid musician, golfer, Pittsburgh sports fan, and "self-proclaimed pizza connoisseur".

You can view more of Brian's work online at www.briandumm.com
