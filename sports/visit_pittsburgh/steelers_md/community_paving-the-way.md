Presented by 

## Paving the Way, Presented by The Pavement Group

#### **For every field goal and extra point our offense records this season, The Pavement Group will donate $500 per field goal and $100 per extra point to Best of the Batch Foundation.**

Advertising

### **2025 Season**

### **2024 Recap**

At the final home game of the regular season, Charlotte Heyward-Wesley, Executive Director of The Heyward House (left) accepted a check from Brian Hess (middle), CEO, and Tre Tipton (right) Coordinator of Strategic Partnerships, of The Pavement Group. The check represented the donation to date during the 2024 season.

### **2023 Recap**

Charlie Batch (middle), founder of Best of the Batch Foundation and former Steelers quarterback, is presented with a check from Brian Hess (left) and Tre Tipton (right) of The Pavement Group at the 2023 home finale, thanks to the contributions from The Pavement Group. Best of the Batch Foundation was the recipient of donations made from Paving the Way in 2023.
