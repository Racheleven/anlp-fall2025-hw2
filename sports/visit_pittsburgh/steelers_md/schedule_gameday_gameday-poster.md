# Pittsburgh Steelers Gameday Poster | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

The Pittsburgh Steelers, in collaboration with Commonwealth Press, release gameday posters designed by locally sourced artists for each home game of the season, and one to commemorate the season in its entirety! A limited number of posters will be available for purchase on game day at the Steelers Pro Shop and online.

**Bio:** Shelby Kirk has been working in Pittsburgh as a designer and illustrator for Bootstrap Design Company for six years. This has allowed her to do illustration work for many beloved Pittsburgh brands such as La Prima Espresso Co., Slice on Broadway, CMU School of Computer Science, Leona's, and Shorty's Pins x Pints. Professional design work challenges her to develop a wide array of styles tailored to the unique voice of each individual client. This project was an exciting opportunity to tap into her own personal style and express her love for the city she calls home.

Week 2: Steelers vs. Seahawks

**Bio:** Jeremy Beightol is an illustrator and painter that got his start in art as a teenager designing t-shirts for himself and his friends in Erie, PA. This small start gained momentum after a short while, catching the attention of various action sports and streetwear brands nationally, then internationally. Steady work allotted him the time to take up painting as a way to break up the monotony of designing, and his work was soon showing in galleries in Pittsburgh and then around the States in solo and group exhibits. Since moving to Pittsburgh in 2009, he has continued to work for clients in a multitude of mediums and applications, including branding, comic book illustration, and murals just to name a few. Most recently, he has put his focus towards working with music acts, designing album covers, merch, and concert posters for bands around the world, but mainly in the Pittsburgh area.

2025 Season Poster

**Bio:** Brian Dumm is a professional artist and art educator from Ebensburg, Pennsylvania where he works and lives with his wife and two daughters. Brian is represented by Storybook Arts Inc. in New York, and his award-winning illustrations have been featured in over a hundred publications globally since 2010. He has completed artwork for such notable clients as Scholastic, McGraw-Hill, and the Sheetz corporation.

Brian earned a Bachelor of Arts degree from Penn State University in 2000 and a master's degree in Drawing and Painting from Indiana University of Pennsylvania in 2013. His fine-arts drawing/paintings have shown in galleries through Pennsylvania, Ohio, and Maryland.

Brian is a member of the Pittsburgh Society of Illustrators, The Allied Artists of Johnstown, and the Pennsylvania Art Education Association. Brian's work is largely illustrative, having been inspired by his earliest visual passions for comic book art, skateboard graphics, and album covers.

Brian is also an avid musician, golfer, Pittsburgh sports fan, and "self-proclaimed pizza connoisseur".

You can view more of Brian's work online at www.briandumm.com

ABOUT THE BENEFICIARY:

Artists Image Resource (AIR) is a laboratory designed to serve artists, educators, and the community. AIR creates an exciting and dynamic learning environment that fosters experimentation, individuality, and peer mentorship by bringing established and emerging artists together in its print studio and exhibition space on Pittsburgh's Northside. AIR's primary activities are to initiate and facilitate projects with professional artists and create spaces where artists, students and the public interact.
