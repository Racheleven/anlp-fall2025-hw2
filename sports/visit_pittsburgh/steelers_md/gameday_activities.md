# Pittsburgh Steelers Game Day Activities | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

**FEDEX AIR & GROUND ZONE**

Located outside of Gate A, the new **44 X 25-yard Football Field** provides a real football feel for all ages, housing the FedEx Air & Ground Zone with various football drills and activities.

**ART ROONEY AVENUE**

Art Rooney Avenue is the hub for all things Steelers pregame. Come join us at this interactive experience for fans of all ages.

**Photo Opportunities -** Take selfies with our Steelers jersey display or compare your size with current & former players!

**Entertainment** \- Have some fun before kickoff!

  * Get a FREE face painting at any Gamefaces station outside of the stadium during pregame!
  * Hang out with Steely McBeam
  * Test your speed at the new 40 Yard Dash activation!
  * Play Plinko at the Steelers tent for your chance to win prizes and giveaways!
  * Explore our Hall of Honor Museum display
  * Visit the Draft Mobile Exhibit as we prepare for 2026

  * The first 300 guests who are 21+ will receive a complimentary Bud Light from the Bud Light Backyard. Fans can enjoy lawn games and purchase other Anheuser-Busch products for sale (Admission is free!)



**STAGE AE**

Stage AE is the official Tailgate Zone of the Pittsburgh Steelers. All tailgates are hosted by Bill Crawford of the DVE Morning Show, and admission is FREE!

**Photo Opportunities -** Â Take photos with friends in front of the famous "YOU'RE IN STEELERS COUNTRY" banner

**Entertainment â**

  * Enjoy great food and drink specials
  * Listen to live music & radio broadcasts from WDVE & iHeart stations
  * Play cornhole, ladder ball, and test your skills at our QB Challenge
  * Win official Steelers prizes and autographed items
  * Watch the game on Stage AE's giant screens!



_Towel Unfurl Check-In will take place at Stage AE._

**FEDEX GREAT HALL**

Watch the live pregame show--Steelers Kickoff, explore the Steelers Pro Shop, with access to concessions all in the FedEx Great Hall.

Location: Enter through East side of Stadium  
Time: Pregame Show - 30 minutes prior to kickoff

**Photo Opportunities** â Flex on your friends by taking photos with our SIX Lombardi trophies, as well as the historic locker and retired jersey displays!

**Food & Bev** â Eat from one of the many concession areas, including Franco's Pizza, Quaker Steak & Lube, and more

**Entertainment** -

  * Participate in Steelers Legend Q&As



**FORD FAN ZONE**

Looking for a spot to hang out with Steelers Nation? Visit the south plaza inside Acrisure Stadium just beneath the scoreboard!

**Photo Opportunities -** Experience the city of Pittsburgh as your backdrop, or snap a picture with the theme backdrops and Steelers logo

**Food & Bev** \- Eat from one of the multiple concessions areas
