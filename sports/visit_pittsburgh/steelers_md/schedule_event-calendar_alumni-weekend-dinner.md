# Steelers Alumni Weekend Dinner | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

This year's Alumni Weekend Dinner presented by U. S. Steel, UPMC and UPMC Health Plan, **_themed around the_ 20th anniversary of** **_Super Bowl XL_** will bring former players, coaches, and staff members back to Pittsburgh to share iconic behind-the-scenes stories and even funny anecdotes from their time in the Black and Gold.

_This event is currently**SOLD OUT** \- subscribe to email to be notified of any future ticket opportunities and/or visit this page for the most up-to-date information._

#### Saturday, November 1

#### **6:00 - 9:30 PM**

#### **Acrisure Stadium**

CONTENT & COVERAGE

###  PHOTOS: 2024 Alumni Weekend Dinner 

The Pittsburgh Steelers celebrated the 50th anniversary of Super Bowl IX during the Alumni Weekend Dinner at Acrisure Stadium benefitting the UPMC Sports Medicine Concussion Program

###  PHOTOS: Members of Super Bowl IX team tour Hall of Honor Museum 

As part of Alumni Weekend and the celebration of the 50th anniversary of Super Bowl IX, members of the 1974 Steelers toured the Hall of Honor Museum

###  WATCH: Full 2023 Alumni Weekend Program 

Watch the full 2023 Alumni Weekend Dinner program that brought together players from the previous two decades of Steelers football

###  WATCH: Steelers 2023 Alumni Weekend recap 

On Saturday night, there was a family reunion when the Steelers hosted the 2023 Alumni Weekend Dinner, presented by UPMC, UPMC Health Plan & U.S. Steel at Acrisure Stadium

###  PHOTOS: 2023 Alumni Weekend Dinner 

The Pittsburgh Steelers celebrated the 2000s and 2010s during the Alumni Weekend Dinner at Acrisure Stadium benefitting the UPMC Sports Medicine Concussion Program

###  Coming home again 

The Steelers 2023 Alumni Weekend Dinner was a homecoming for players from the last few decades

###  Steelers honor alumni at halftime 

As a part of the 90th season celebration, the Steelers honored alumni from every decade during halftime of Sunday's game

###  PHOTOS: 2022 Alumni Weekend Dinner
