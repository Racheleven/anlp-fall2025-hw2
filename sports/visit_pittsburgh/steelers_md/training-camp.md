STEELERS TRAINING CAMP

Join the team at Saint Vincent College, where admission to open practices is FREE, but you MUST have a mobile ticket. Attendees can participate in family activities, take advantage of photo ops, get gear direct from the team at the Steelers Pro Shop, and more.

**_Fans are encouraged to arrive at parking lots when they open at 10:45 AM. Entry will not be available before the 11 AM gate open time - stadium access opens at 12 PM._**

_Date and times subject to change. Practice may be cancelled or moved due to inclement weather.Download the Steelers Official Mobile App to opt in to Training Camp push notifications (Settings > Notifications), and/or follow along on all Steelers digital platforms, for the latest weather and practice updates should they become available._

CAMP FEATURES

### 

Practice and Seating

Enjoy an amphitheater-like experience at Saint Vincent College as your favorite Steelers take the field for practice! Bring your chairs, blankets or whatever seating accommodations you prefer to kick off the season with us!

### 

Player Interactions

While autographs are not guaranteed to fans attending Training Camp, we encourage fans to arrive when gates open to find seating closer to the field level. There is no designated area for this.

### 

Fan Activities & Prizes

Here's some of what you can experience at our 2025 Training Camp:

  * Win prizes at our Steelers tent
  * Participate in football drills in the FedEx Air & Ground Zone
  * Say hello to Steely McBeam all around campus!



### 

Steelers Pro Shop

Get your gear direct from the Team at the Official Steelers Pro Shop location at Training Camp. You will find the latest gear and Steelers Memorabilia for the whole family! _*Steelers Pro Shop gift cards will not be accepted at Training Camp locations._

### 

Concessions

A variety of food trucks will be available on campus for fans throughout the course of practice. Some vendors are cash-only.

### 

Steelers Legend Meet & Greets

Each day of open practice, fans can visit the Hall of Honor Museum display for photo opportunities with Steelers Legends.

KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

#### 

Camp Entrance

Fans should enter through Saint Vincent Drive to the parking lot and follow camp directional signage to the camp entrance.

**10:45 AM: Parking lots open**

**11:00 AM: Gates open to Pro Shop tent & fan activities**

**12:00 PM: Gates open to stadium seating**

**1:55 PM: Practice begins**

Upon entry to camp, please have your mobile ticke**t** ready to scan at the gates.

#### 

Parking

Free parking is available for all fans visiting Steelers Training Camp. The parking lot entrance is located at the intersection of Saint Vincent Drive and Brouwers Road and is clearly marked by camp directional signage.

*Weekend practices bring larger crowds - expect traffic congestion and longer walks from available parking.

ADA parking can be found in the Fred Rogers Center Parking Lot on 300 Fraser Purchase Rd, Latrobe, PA 15650. Once you have arrived on campus, there will be signage directing you into the designated ADA lot. Fans MUST use the shuttle when parking in ADA/Rogers Center Parking spaces to get to campus.

*You must have a Disability Parking Placard to be permitted.

#### 

Mobile Ticketing

Fans can secure their free digital tickets through Ticketmaster. All practices will require a free ticket for your entry, and registration will be available on a first-come, first-served basis. For how to access your digital tickets, how to forward tickets to others and how to accept forwarded tickets from others, please visit our Mobile Ticketing page.

#### 

Security and Bag Policy

The safety of our fans is essential, and we're committed to ensuring your Training Camp experience is second to none. The Pittsburgh Steelers and Saint Vincent College have implemented a safety policy, making the security process more efficient and effective. We encourage you to review the prohibited items list in our FAQs. **_Alcohol is NOT permitted at Steelers Training Camp or around the Saint Vincent College Campus._**
