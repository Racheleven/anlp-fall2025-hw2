#### CLUB AND LEXUS LOGE

Previous Previous

/ 

fullscreen

ANNUAL LEASE

  * Four (4) or eight (8) tickets to all regular and preseason Penguins home games and available non-Penguins PPG Paints Arena events (Club Loge presented by Lexus) or right of first refusal to purchase non-Penguins PPG Paints Arena events
