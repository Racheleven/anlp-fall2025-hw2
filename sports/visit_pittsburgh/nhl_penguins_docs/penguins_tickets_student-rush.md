### Score discounted tickets with GetGo Student Rush all season long!

GetGo Student Rush is back for the 2025.26 season! As a reminder, you must be a college student with a valid .edu email address to unlock special pricing. Tickets cannot be transferred or resold, and all guests associated with the purchase must enter PPG Paints Arena together!

GetGo Student Rush tickets for Friday, October 3 versus the Buffalo Sabres will go on sale game day at 10 AM.

IMPORTANT NOTE: Students will be placed in a queue upon clicking the BUY NOW link below. Please stay on this page until your access begins!

## Enable Push Notifications

The Penguins app is fantastic for keeping up with the team! Be sure to enable "Student Rush Promotions" push notifications to be notified of unique student offerings. You can use the the Penguins app to access your tickets, follow the team, and more!
