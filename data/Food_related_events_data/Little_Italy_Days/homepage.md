- The Miss Little Italy is a family-oriented festival pageant, celebrating a special Bloomfield tradition.  
- Contestants **DO NOT** need to be of Italian heritage or reside in the city of Pittsburgh.  
- **Event Fee:** $35.00 for first child. Includes a participation gift for all.  
- Miss Little Italy Winners receive a crown, sash & flowers.
