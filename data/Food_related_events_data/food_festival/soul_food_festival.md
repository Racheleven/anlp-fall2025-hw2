Soul Food Festival
==================

Savor a soulful taste of the Burgh at Pittsburgh’s can’t miss Soul Food Festival!

[Soul Food Festival](#jlsoul-food-festival)

[Entertainment Schedule](#jlentertainment-schedule)

Spice up your [Labor Day weekend](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/labor-day-weekend-in-pittsburgh/) at [Pittsburgh's Soul Food Festival](https://m.facebook.com/ASoulfulTasteOfTheBurgh/) – a mouthwatering celebration of the city's Black culinary, butchery, and food businesses. Established in 2019, this festival honors Pittsburgh's rich history of food service pioneers and entrepreneurs, dating back to 1795 when Charles Richards opened the city's first [Black-owned](https://www.visitpittsburgh.com/blog/black-owned-pittsburgh/) restaurant and lodge. Spend the weekend feasting on soul food dishes from more than 100 small business vendors and food service proprietors, while enjoying live entertainment and engaging activities.

Soul Food Festival
------------------

### Event Details

*   **Dates:** August 29 - 31, 2025
*   **Time:** 11 a.m. - 11 p.m. Daily
*   **Location:** Stanwix Street and Liberty Avenue in Downtown Pittsburgh
*   **Admission:** FREE

### Soul Food & Good Times

Indulge in a feast for the senses as soul food vendors serve up irresistible dishes. Witness pitmasters battle it out in the sizzling BBQ contest, explore rich cultural activities, and peruse unique crafts from talented vendors. As the aroma of soul food fills the air, visit the stages and dance the night away.
