##  Upcoming Events 

All Events 

Fri Oct 10 | 07:00 PM - 10:00 PM

Steelers Style

Thank you for your interest in Steelers Style, presented by Ford, UPMC & UPMC Health Plan. More information for the 2025 event will be live soon!  
This event is our largest and most anticipated fundraising event of the year! Watch our players and special guests walk the runway to raise money for Steelers Charities, a 5013C that supports the UPMC Sports Medicine Concussion Program, Cancer Bridges and the Chuck Noll Foundation.

Stage AE

Learn More

Sat Nov 01 | 06:00 PM - 09:00 PM

Steelers Alumni Weekend Dinner

This year's Alumni Weekend Dinner, **_themed around the_ 20th anniversary of** **_Super Bowl XL_** will bring former players, coaches, and staff members back to Pittsburgh to share iconic behind-the-scenes stories and even funny anecdotes from their time in the Black and Gold.

Acrisure Stadium

Learn More Buy Tickets 

Sun Dec 14 | 05:30 PM - 09:00 PM

Hall of Honor Dinner Ceremony

Our annual Hall of Honor Dinner celebrates the induction of each year's Hall of Honor class during a special ceremony at Acrisure Stadium. The evening features drinks, dinner, and the appearance of Steelers Legends.

This is a Steelers Charities event, and ticket proceeds benefit The Chuck Noll Foundation for Brain Injury Research.
