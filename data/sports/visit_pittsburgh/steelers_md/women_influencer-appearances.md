# Pittsburgh Steelers Women Influencer Appearances | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

INFLUENCERS & CELEBS OF STEEL

#### Women of Steel is getting a boost of star power, with our local celebrities and influencers showing up in numbers at Steelers games and events! They're not just cheering for the team; they're also showing Steelers Nation that community spirit & female empowerment are always in style!

#### 

Annie Rodgers

Annie Rodgers, a lifelong Steelers fan and foodie content creator, has an arsenal of game-day recipes. She's excited to share her most popular recipe with Steelers Nation to help us prepare for game day!

WATCH! 

#### 

Gabrielle Payne

Gabrielle Payne brought her followers along for a night of glitz and grit at Steelers Style, the team's annual charity fashion show! She rocked her Steelers look and joined in on an unforgettable evening of fashion and football in black and gold.

WATCH! 

#### 

Sydney Carver Snyder

Sydney Carver Snyder joined the team at Steelers Style and took her followers along for the glam! From runway moments to celebrating a great cause, she showed us all how to rock that Steelers spirit.

WATCH! 

#### 

Kendall Vertes

Kendall Vertes came back to the 'Burgh to cheer on her hometown team under the lights! Nothing like that Steelers pride at home!

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

Diana Flores

Diana Flores, quarterback of Mexico women's flag football team, met with Pittsburgh Steelers Cam Heyward to talk about the everlasting impact women have in sports, both on and off the field.

WATCH! 

#### 

Francesca Ferrante

Steelers super fan and Pittsburgh influencer, Francesca Ferrante, brought the energy to Training Camp by surprising two amazing Women of Steel with the opportunity to win sideline passes! They experienced practice up close and felt the magic of the game like never before.

WATCH! 

#### 

Lacey Jane Brown

Lacey Jane Brown toured the Hall of Honor Museum prior to joining Steelers Nation for Thursday Night Football against the Tennessee Titans on November 2nd as part of her Primetime Tour.

SEE THE POST!  WATCH THE TIKTOK! 

#### 

Brooke Hyland

Former dancer and reality-TV star, Brooke Hyland, surprised her die-hard Steelers fan boyfriend with tickets to a Steelers home game against the Ravens on October 8th.

WATCH THE TIKTOK!  WATCH THE TIKTOK! 

#### 

Chloe Xandria

Chloe Xandria, a local fashion influencer, joined the annual Pittsburgh Steelers Rock Steelers Style charitable fashion show and Steelers home game against the Cleveland Browns on September 18th.

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

Britt Baker

AEW wrestler and die-hard Steelers fan, Brit Baker, came to a Steelers home game on October 29th as part of a friendly competition with her manager, a partial owner of the Jaguars.

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

GloRilla

GloRilla, Rapper and life-long Steeler fan, finally got to meet Head Coach Mike Tomlin and tour the practice facility during her visit to Pittsburgh.

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

Carmella

WWE wrestler, Carmella, and her husband were surprised with personalized jerseys from Cam Heyward to welcome their future Steelers fan at training camp.

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

AnaEats

Yinzer and Steeler fan, Ana, stopped by a game to tailgate and check out some Acrisure Stadium-exclusive drinks.

SEE THE POST!  SEE THE POST! 

#### 

Abbie Herbert

Viral TikTok Mom star, Abbie Herbert, and her husband cheered on the Steelers against the Ravens at Acrisure Stadium on October 8th.

SEE THE POST! 

#### 

Emily Harrigan

Former Pitt soccer player, Emily Harrigan, created a viral TikTok where she attempted a 50-yard field goal at Acrisure Stadium during the Pittsburgh Steelers home opener on September 10th.

WATCH THE TIKTOK! 

#### 

Lex Del Valle

Lex Del Valle brought her Steelers fan-filled family to our home game on December 23rd against the Cincinnati Bengals. As a part of our "Thank You Fans" initiative, she was able to hand out pregame field passes to 2 lucky gameday attendees!

SEE THE POST!  SEE THE POST!
