# Pittsburgh Steelers Field Seats | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

#### Steelers Field Seats are officially SOLD OUT for the 2025 season! To learn more or join the waitlist for Field Seats, please enter your information below. You can also contact us directly at 412-785-7773 or sth@steelers.nfl.com

REQUEST INFORMATION 

#### **_"We're committed to developing new and exciting seating products at Acrisure Stadium, and our field seats are the latest addition. These seats bring you closer to the game than ever before, providing a brand-new perspective and a once-in-a-lifetime experience." - Ryan Huzjak, Vice President Sales & Marketing_**

##  BENEFITS OF FIELD SEATS 

#### 

Premium Hospitality

All-inclusive food and beverage included within a private, concourse-level lounge.

#### 

Luxury Seating

Watch the game while in the best seats in the stadium. Seating includes extra legroom and wider seats.

#### 

VIP Parking

Complimentary Parking at the Stage AE lot right across from Acrisure Stadium and a VIP entrance dedicated to these seats.

#### 

Front-Row Experience

Feel like a part of the game with seats feet away from the field, an experience that cannot be matched.
