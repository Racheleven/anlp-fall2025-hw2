# Pittsburgh Steelers Gameday Poster | Pittsburgh Steelers - Steelers.com

#### **The Pittsburgh Steelers, in collaboration with Commonwealth Press, are releasing 8 game day posters, one for each home game during the 2022 season. Local artists sourced by Commonwealth Press will design each poster to commemorate the individual game. A limited number of posters will be available for purchase on game day at the Steelers Pro Shop.**

_All gameday poster proceeds will benefitArtists Image Resource located in Pittsburgh._

**ABOUT THE BENEFICIARY:** Artists Image Resource (AIR)is a laboratory designed to serve artists, educators, and the community. AIR creates an exciting and dynamic learning environment that fosters experimentation, individuality, and peer mentorship by bringing established and emerging artists together in its print studio and exhibition space on Pittsburgh's Northside. AIR's primary activities are to initiate and facilitate projects with professional artists and create spaces where artists, students and the public interact. **MORE...**

Week 17: Steelers vs. Browns

**Artist:** Rick Bach

**Bio:** Rick Bach is an American painter, sculptor and graphic designer. Originally from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, and a graduate from the Pittsburgh Art Institute, his style was influenced by his early days working alongside his father in an auto body shop. His work ranges from architectural to figurative and is usually painted on metal canvases or sculpted from steel. His motivation is to entertain and provoke while pushing the boundaries between conventional and absurd, functional and useless. Bach currently resides in Washington, D.C., and maintains a studio there as well as in Pittsburgh. For more than 20 years, he has been commissioned by the Big Burrito Restaurant Group to create site-specific murals and sculptures for their restaurants throughout the east coast. Bach's work is in private collections around the world, and he has been featured in numerous solo and group exhibitions. Week 15: Steelers vs. Titans

**Artist:** Angela Oddling

**Bio:** Angela Oddling is a self-taught illustrator, comics creator, and traditional artist from Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania. Her illustrations are created with whimsical colors and designs to bring characters and stories to life. She hopes the art that she creates can help folks from all walks of life find some comfort. Angela spends her free time dancing in the kitchen with her cats Daisy and Luke, telling ghost stories, and diner-hopping on her quest to find the world's best oatmeal. **Artist:** Shawn Atkins

**Bio:** Shawn Atkins is a cartoonist on webcomics and records podcasts. He is a lover of oatmeal raisin cookies and his dad. Comics are his lifelong passion. Week 10: Steelers vs. Lions

**Artist:** Abbie Adams

**Bio:** Abbie Adams is a Pittsburgh-based artist and designer. Adams works with ink and gouache, as well as digital illustration and collage. The simplicity and detail of her imagery define her signature aesthetic and approach, whether working on a mural or a house portrait. Abbie Adams Studio has collaborated with a variety of businesses to create distinctive, locally-themed products and projects. Her love of architecture, repetition, and subtle imperfection influences almost everything she creates. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)

Week 9: Steelers vs. Bears

**Artist:** Nina Zivkovic

**Bio:** Nina Zivkovic is an art director, designer, and illustrator at a South Side studio called ocreations. She also freelances to musicians, actors, and entertainment professionals. From national sports nutrition companies to local restaurants and staples, Nina has a vase variety of design & illustrator industry experience, citing entertainment as her favorite: "Illustration and design are like being an actor. You study and research and express yourself through a focused lens, to capture the right emotions and theme. With entertainment illustration clients, you get to get super weird and creative, and with graphic design at ocreations you have rules, goals, and structure. Two different types of challenges, both of which I love." She is continually inspired by art nouveau, comic books, and movie posters, and is an avid football and all things geek-nerd fan. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)

Week 6: Steelers vs. Seahawks

**Artist:** Mike Pfaltzgraff

**Bio:** My name is Michael Pfaltzgraff. I am a Pittsburgh-based graphic designer. I've been a designer for over 25 years. Growing up in the Pacific Northwest, I had an interest in bikes at a young age. This had a significant influence on my interest in design. I got an early start designing for the bike industry when I was still in high school. Since then, I've been a designer by trade and have worked with some of the most established outdoor companies in the world. There are over 3 million bikes out in the world with my design work on them. For the last ten years I've been running a small, independent design agency based in Pittsburgh. From The North Face to the Carnegie Museum of Natural History, I have worked with a variety of brands on projects that are creatively fulfilling. Notable clients and companies I have worked with include:

Design Direction - Specialized Bikes / Trek Bikes / Gary Fisher Bikes / Marin Bikes.

Independent Projects - The North Face/ Olympics / Sealy - Tempurpedic Beds / Cannondale Bikes / Fuji Bikes / Rossignol Skis

Illustration - Wired Magazine / Fast Company

(Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)

Week 5: Steelers vs. Broncos

**Artist:** Strawberry Luna

**Bio:** Starting as a hobby, Strawberryluna is the wife and husband design team of Allison Glancey and Craig Seder. Based in Pittsburgh, they became a creative team in 2005 and quickly went from designing rock posters for bands you 've never heard of (and some of you never will), to designing, illustrating, and creating visuals for some of the largest brands (and bands) in the world. From designs for bands like The National and Belle & Sebastian to working for The TODAY Show to Three Rivers Arts Festival, Strawberryluna loves creating strong, eye-catching designs. Getting to design today's Game Day Poster is a dream come true for these two lifelong Steelers fans. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)

Week 2: Steelers vs. Raiders

**Artist:** Mark Bender

**Bio:** Mark Bender's illustration is classically inspired and hand-crafted, combining traditional media with contemporary technology to create compelling imagery that connects with the audience. His visual vocabulary pulls from vintage advertising of the 20's and 30's WPA and propaganda posters, giving contemporary concepts a timeless motif. Mark's meticulously crafted images have been incorporated into advertising, editorial and institutional clients including Simon & Shuster, Sony Music, NPR, UPMC Children's Hospital of Pittsburgh, Heinz USA, Carnegie Mellon, PITT University, NFL, MLB, East End Brewing and Federated Investors. Recognized by Communication Arts Magazine, 3X3 Magazine, Archive 200 Best Illustrators, Applied Arts Magazine, Creative Quarterly, the Los Angeles Society of Illustrators and the New York Society of Illustrators, Mark has been included in the Society's annual exhibitions and in Original Art, the best in children's book illustration. A member of the AIGA and a founding member of the Pittsburgh Society of Illustrators, he has also been awarded both gold and silver Addys, Golden Quill Awards, two PSI Best of Show awards, a gold medal from the Independent Publishers Book Awards, and silver medals from both the New York Society of Illustrators and the Los Angeles Society of Illustrators. Three of Mark's works are in the permanent collection of the Society of Illustrators Museum in New York. Mark has been passing on his illustration knowledge as an educator in Pittsburgh for over 2 decades and is an Associate Professor in Graphic Design at Chatham University. (Poster curated by CommonWealth Press; Photo credit: Matt Dayak)
