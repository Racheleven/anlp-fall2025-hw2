### LEGEND

  * W  : Wins 
  * L  : Losses 
  * T  : Ties 
  * PCT  : Winning Percentage 
  * HOME  : Home Record 
  * ROAD  : Road Record 


  * DIV  : Division Record 
  * CONF  : Conference Record 
  * PF  : Total Points For 
  * PA  : Total Points Against 
  * NET PTS  : Net Points 
  * STRK  : Streak 


  * x  : Clinched Playoff 
  * y  : Clinched Wild Card 
  * z  : Clinched Division 
  * *  : Clinched Division and Homefield Advantage
