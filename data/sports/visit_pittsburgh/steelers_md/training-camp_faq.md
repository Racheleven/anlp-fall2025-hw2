##  Steelers Training Camp FAQ - 2025 

**Q: What does it cost to attend a Steelers Training Camp at Saint Vincent College?**

A: Admission is FREE to all Steelers Training Camp practices at Saint Vincent College. Fans will need to have a mobile ticket to enter Saint Vincent College.

**Q: How can I get a ticket to Steelers Training Camp?**

A: To secure a ticket to Steelers Training Camp CLICK HERE >>>

**Q: How can I get a ticket to Steelers Training Camp if I don't have a smartphone?**

A: Secure your ticket online for the dates that you want to attend, and a staff member will help you troubleshoot on the day of practice.

**Q: What time does Steelers Practice start?**

A: 10:45 AM: Parking lots open

11:00 AM: Gates open to Pro Shop tent & fan activities

12:00 PM: Gates open to stadium seating

1:55 PM: Practice begins

**Q: Are all Training Camp practices open to the public?**

A: All "Open Practices" are available to fans. For the full schedule, visit our Training Camp website for more information. Morning walk-throughs are closed to the public.

**Q: How can I attend the Friday Night Lights practice?**

A: Tickets for Friday Night Lights can be purchased by visiting https://www.steelers.com/schedule/event-calendar/friday-night-lights/. All proceeds from this event will benefit the Latrobe Greater School District.

**PARKING AND BAG POLICY**

**Q: Where can I park at Saint Vincent College?**

A: Free parking is available for all fans visiting Steelers Training Camp. The parking lot entrance is located at the intersection of Saint Vincent Drive and Brouwers Road and is clearly marked by camp directional signage.

_*Weekend practices bring larger crowds - expect traffic congestion and longer walks from available parking._

ADA parking can be found in the Fred Rogers Center Parking Lot on 300 Fraser Purchase Rd, Latrobe, PA 15650. Once you have arrived on campus, there will be signage directing you into the designated ADA lot. All fans parking in the Fred Rogers Center MUST take the provided shuttle to campus.

_*You must have a Disability Parking Placard to be permitted._

**Q: Where can I park my RV or trailer at training camp?**

A: Fans can enter the parking lot entrance at the intersection of Saint Vincent Drive and Brouwers Road. It is clearly marked by camp directional signage. Space for RVs and trailers is based on availability.

**Q: Can I tailgate at Steelers Training Camp?**

A: Tailgating is not permitted on the Saint Vincent College property. Tents, grill tables and other tailgating equipment are not permitted on campus. Fans are welcome to bring their own chairs, as seating space in the bleachers facing the Chuck Noll Field is limited based on first-come first-serve basis.

**Q: Is there a Clear Bag Policy at Steelers Training Camp?**

A: No. There is not a Clear Bag Policy at Steelers Training Camp.

**Q: What can I NOT bring to Steelers Training Camp?**

A: As mandated by the Steelers Training Camp security and Saint Vincent College, the following items are NOT permitted on campus:

  * Firearms, weapons of any type or fireworks
  * Alcohol
  * Video cameras or professional cameras/lenses
  * Audio recording devices
  * Flammable liquids
  * Noisemakers - air horns, klaxon, bells, whistles, etc.
  * Noise enhancing devices
  * Stereo systems
  * Laser pointers of any type, poles, or rods
  * Any other items deemed dangerous or unacceptable by security
  * Smoking of any kind, no vapes
  * For more questions on permitted vs. non-permitted items, please call (412) 323-1200



**FOOD AND BEVERAGE / MERCHANDISE**

**Q: Can I purchase food and beverages at Steelers Training Camp?**

A: A variety of food trucks will be available to fans throughout the course of practice. Some vendors are cash-only.

**Q: Can I bring or purchase alcohol for Steelers Training Camp?**

A: No alcohol is permitted at Steelers Training Camp or around the Saint Vincent College Campus. Violators will be escorted off the property.

**Q: Will I be able to purchase merchandise and memorabilia at the Steelers Pro Shop?**

A: Yes. The Steelers Pro Shop and Steelers Authentic Memorabilia will be located at the entrance of camp where fans can purchase official player jerseys, autographed items, and more.

**RAIN/WEATHER DELAYS**

**Q: What happens if there is inclement weather?**

A: Download the Steelers Official Mobile App to opt in to Training Camp push notifications (Settings > Notifications), and/or follow along on all Steelers digital platforms, for the latest weather and practice updates should they become available.

**MISCELLANEOUS**

**Q: Can I get autographs at Steelers Training Camp?**

A: While autographs are not guaranteed to fans attending Training Camp, we encourage fans to arrive early to find seating closer to the field level. There is no designated area for this.

**Q: Where can I access a restroom?**

A: Several restroom facilities are available around Steelers Training Camp.

**Q: Can I bring my pet to Training Camp?**

A: Pets are not permitted at Steelers Training Camp. Exceptions are made only for service animals.

**Q: Can I film practice?**

A: No, fans cannot film practice. No audio or video recording is permitted on any device.

**Emergency and Special Assistance**

  * Local EMS, campus security and law enforcement representatives are available on campus. For immediate assistance either identify a campus security representative or call 911.



_*For additional questions regarding Steelers Training Camp, please call our offices at_ (412) 323-1200.
